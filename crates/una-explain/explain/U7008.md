# U7008: Invalid `ita.una` or `itaty.una`

From edition `0.4`, a package describes itself in `ita.una`, and a workspace in `itaty.una` (ADR 0122, `spec/30`). Both are written in Una's own lexical syntax, by a closed grammar: a head `ita <name>` with its `version` and `edition` lines, then sections in a fixed order (`sources`, `needs`, `replace`, `trust`, `labels`, `trusted`, `escapes`, `boundaries`, `contracts`, `run`, `drive`, `serve`). Nothing in them is evaluated: there are no expressions, no interpolation and no includes. U7008 reports, at its line, a syntax error, an unknown, repeated or misplaced line or section, a value of the wrong form or out of range, an edition before `0.4`, a directory that holds both `ita.una` and `una.toml`, a reserved local name, a `from git` origin, a `foreign` section or grant, an invalid limit line under a grant (a form other than `at most <n> per second`, `minute` or `hour`, `at most <n> at once` and `waiting at most <n>`, a count below 1 or above 2147483647, a limit under a grant other than `http`, or a kind of limit given twice; ADR 0117 rule 8, `spec/27 §9`), every error that `una.toml` reports as U7002 for the setting it corresponds to, and a `requires`, or a refined parameter or result type, on `handle` or `on_stop` of the module a `serve` section names, a precondition that no call of the host establishes (`spec/31 §7`).

## Failing example

`ita.una`

```una
ita demo
  version 0.1
  edition 0.4
```

`ita.lock`

```una
lock 1

solver
  encoding "una-smt-1"
  name "z3"
  version "5.1.0"
  logic "QF_UFLIA"
  steps 5000000
  seed 0
```

`src/demo.una`

```una
module demo

pub one() -> Int = 1
```
