# U7014: A locked release yanked, under an advisory or signed after a stated compromise, or a pending recovery, a key change or a transfer of its owner

`una ita audit` checks the registry packages of `ita.lock` against each name's state at a fresh checkpoint of its registry, verified as every registry document is (ADR 0122 rule 41, `spec/30 §15`). U7014 reports, once per package with every finding, a locked release that its owner has yanked; one that an advisory names; one signed by a key that was later revoked as compromised since a log entry before it; and, since the lock's resolution point, a recovery pending for its owner or a change of its owner's keys. A lock keeps a yanked release, and `una build` stays offline and never calls the audit, so a project's release gate runs `una ita audit` and fails on U7014. The repair is to raise the package with `una ita update <name>`, which takes the next release that is neither yanked nor advised, or to find out why the owner's keys changed before taking anything new. `una check` does not report U7014. The examples below are audited against a registry `acme` whose state has `acme/ledger 0.3.1` yanked and `0.3.2` released, neither advised.

A package of edition `0.4` locks each registry release with the key that signed it (ADR 0122 rules 25 and 38, `spec/30 §14`). The keys of a package's owner are the ones its chain in the registry's log gives at each log position: keys added and revoked, a threshold, and recoveries and changes of recovery co-signers, which take effect only seven days of witness time after they were logged, unless a current key cancels them. `una ita lock` and `una ita update` compare the owner's keys with the ones the lock was written under: another signing key, or a key event of the owner logged after the lock's resolution point, a recovery or a co-signer change that took effect included, is a key change, and one still pending is reported too. U7014 reports it at the need, and nothing is written, so a stolen or recovered owner account never changes what a program runs without the programmer's consent. The repair is to review the owner's key events in the registry's log, then to record the change with `una ita lock --accept-key <package>` or `una ita update --accept-key <package>`. A release signed after its key's stated compromise is skipped by new resolutions as a yanked one is.

```una
ita demo
  version 0.1.0
  edition 0.4

sources
  acme "https://ita.acme.example"
    root "sha256:5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e"

needs
  acme/ledger 0.3 from acme
```
