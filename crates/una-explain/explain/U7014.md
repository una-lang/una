# U7014: A locked release yanked, under an advisory or signed after a stated compromise, or a pending recovery, a key change or a transfer of its owner

A package of edition `0.4` locks each registry release with the key that signed it (ADR 0122 rules 25 and 38, `spec/30 §14`). The keys of a package's owner are the ones its chain in the registry's log gives at each log position: keys added and revoked, a threshold, and recoveries, which take effect only seven days of witness time after they were logged, unless a current key cancels them. `una ita lock` and `una ita update` compare the owner's keys with the ones the lock was written under: another signing key, or a key event of the owner logged after the lock's resolution point, a recovery that took effect included, is a key change. U7014 reports it at the need, and nothing is written, so a stolen or recovered owner account never changes what a program runs without the programmer's consent. The repair is to review the owner's key events in the registry's log, then to record the change with `una ita lock --accept-key <package>` or `una ita update --accept-key <package>`. A release signed after its key's stated compromise is skipped by new resolutions as a yanked one is. `una check` does not report U7014: it reads the lock, which records the key, so this text has no example of its own.
