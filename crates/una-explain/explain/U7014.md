# U7014: A locked release that is yanked, under an advisory, or signed after a stated compromise

`una ita audit` checks the registry packages of `ita.lock` against each name's state at a fresh checkpoint of its registry, verified as every registry document is (ADR 0122 rule 41, `spec/30 §14`). U7014 reports, once per package with every finding, a locked release that its owner has yanked; one that an advisory names; one signed by a key that was later revoked as compromised since a log entry before it; and, since the lock's resolution point, a recovery pending for its owner, a change of its owner's keys, or a transfer of its name. A lock keeps a yanked release, and `una build` stays offline and never calls the audit, so a project's release gate runs `una ita audit` and fails on U7014. The repair is to raise the package with `una ita update <name>`, which takes the next release that is neither yanked nor advised, or to find out why the owner's keys changed before taking anything new. `una check` does not report U7014. The examples below are audited against a registry `acme` whose state has `acme/ledger 0.3.1` yanked and `0.3.2` released, neither advised.

## Failing example

`ita.una`

```una
ita demo
  version 0.1.0
  edition 0.4

sources
  acme "https://ita.acme.example"
    root "sha256:5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e"
    owners acme

needs
  acme/ledger 0.3 from acme
```
