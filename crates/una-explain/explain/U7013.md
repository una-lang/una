# U7013: No resolution: no release meets a need, or a bound of resolution is exceeded

`una ita lock`, `una ita update` and `una ita add` resolve a package of edition `0.4` by minimal version selection (ADR 0122 rules 23 to 25, `spec/30 §10`): for each identity, a source, a name and a major line, the highest of the least versions that the needs reached ask for. U7013 reports that there is no resolution, and nothing is written: no release of an identity is at or above a need; a selection must be raised past a yanked, advised or avoided release and no later release qualifies, or the one that would is held back by the cooldown, which `una ita audit` reviews; a pre-release would be selected that no need reached names; a release needs its own identity; a bound of resolution is exceeded, such as 1,000 needs of one release or 10,000 packages in the build list; or a source cannot be resolved offline, since the per-name state of a registry at a resolution point is not held, so no registry is ever resolved from a partial view of it. `una ita verify` reports U7013 too for a registry package whose copies match the lock but which is not verified, since the owner's key chain is not held until the registry client supplies it. The repair is to name a release that exists, to need a version past one that must be raised, to remove the `avoid` line that excludes the only one, or to need fewer packages. `una check` does not report U7013: it reads the lock, and reports a lock that does not meet `ita.una` as U7009. So this text has no example of its own.
