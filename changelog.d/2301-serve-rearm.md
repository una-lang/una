- **A serve thread registers its wake pair and the accept poller again when a registration fails** (#2301, #2302, `spec/31 §6` rules 2 and 13). After the start, a failed re-registration of a serve thread's wake pair, or of the accept poller while the thread holds the turn to accept, was dropped: the thread no longer saw the messages in its inbox (replies, closes, stops) until a timer or a socket woke it, or no longer saw new connections. The thread now registers them again at its next pass and sleeps at most 10 ms meanwhile. New tests force the failure of each re-registration, and of a connection's read registration (which closes the connection at once, as the write registration does since #2279), and check the operating system's error number in the record of a reply cut short. O(1) per pass, as before.
