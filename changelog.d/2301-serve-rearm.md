- **A serve thread registers its wake pair and the accept poller again when a registration fails** (#2301, #2302, #2342, #2343, #2344, `spec/31 §6` rules 2 and 13). After the start, a failed re-registration of a serve thread's wake pair, or of the accept poller while the thread holds the turn to accept, was dropped: the thread no longer saw the messages in its inbox (replies, closes, stops) until a timer or a socket woke it, or no longer saw new connections. The thread now registers them again at each pass and sleeps at most 10 ms meanwhile, however many times the registration fails. A thread whose accept poller fails 100 times in a row (about one second) passes the turn to accept to another serve thread, whose readiness queue may work. The run counts every such failure (`Server::rearm_failures`). New tests force 20 failures in a row of each re-registration, a thread whose accept poller never registers again, and the failure of a connection's read registration (which closes the connection at once, as the write registration does since #2279), and check the operating system's error number in the record of a reply cut short. O(1) per pass, as before.
