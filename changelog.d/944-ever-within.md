- **`for ever within d`, edition 0.4 preview** (#944, ADR 0116 rule 11, IF5 to IF8, rule 21, `spec/26 §7`). Each iteration of the loop has the deadline `d`, an `Int of Millisecond` of at least 1 (U2028, U2001, U5001), after its start; past it, every `Http` operation the iteration waits on or starts fails at once with `TimedOut`, in both engines, and the trace gets one `timeout` event per iteration, with the loop's own task, where the iteration first observes the failure. The failures a deadline causes carry the timing label of the loop's body, which one checker rule now computes for every site with a deadline and for the driven step: a body whose timing depends on data above `internal` is U4001 at the loop. `una report` lists each `for ever within` with its timing label in `information.timing`. A host stop and the deadline cut the same waits, whichever comes first. `within` stays reserved elsewhere.
