- **Soundness: the timing label follows a policy call into its `via` function** (#1673, ADR 0116 IF5, IF6 and P1, `spec/07 §5`). A policy call runs its `via` function, but the timing label's call graph had no edge for it. So a deadline or a race around a policy call did not count what the `via` installs, and a request in a `via` called beside tasks escaped the label of a limited grant. Programs accepted before are now refused: a `within` or a `for ever within` around a policy whose `via` installs a handler with a `secret` parameter (U4001), and a policy whose `via` makes a request on a limited grant, called in a branch of `together`, the block of a `tasks` statement or a branch of `first of` (U4001 at the request).
