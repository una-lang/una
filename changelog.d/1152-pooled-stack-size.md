- **Task stacks sized from a frame bound** (#1152, ADR 0117 amendment of 2026-11-09, proposed; `spec/27 §4` rule 1). `una build` records the largest frame Cranelift laid out for any compiled function, plus linkage and alignment, as the program's frame bound, and an executable sizes each task stack as `16 MiB + depth × bound`, never more than before. At the default `--max-depth` of 10,000 and a typical bound of 128 bytes, the 64 stacks pooled before `main` reserve about 1.1 GiB of address space instead of about 21 GiB. Both inputs are public, so every pooled stack still has one size. A program whose frames the compiler does not state, or whose largest frame is over 64 KiB, keeps the old size, and the interpreter keeps its own.
