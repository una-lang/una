- **Safety: six `unsafe` blocks of `una-sys` become safe code** (#1483, `una-sys`). The socket address that `server::listen` binds is a `sockaddr_in` or `sockaddr_in6` written field by field, in place of a zeroed `sockaddr_storage` read through two pointer casts (3 blocks); the solver child's spawn duplicates a source below descriptor 3 with `BorrowedFd::try_clone_to_owned` and refuses a duplicate that lands below 3, in place of a raw `fcntl` and `OwnedFd::from_raw_fd` (2 blocks); and the event buffer of the reactor starts from a struct literal, in place of `mem::zeroed` (1 block). The system calls, their flags and their errors are unchanged. The trusted modules hold 130 `unsafe` blocks, down from 136 (`docs/security/tcb.md`). Tests: a child whose standard output is the parent's descriptor 0 receives that file, and a listener is bound to the address and port asked for in both families.
