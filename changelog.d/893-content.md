- **Package content, digest and archive** (#893, ADR 0122 rules 27 to 29, `spec/30 §9`). The files a package of edition `0.4` holds (Una sources, `ita.una` and its documents only, with no foreign code), the canonical listing and the content digest that names it, and the uncompressed archive it travels in, with a reader that refuses any path, length, order or hash it does not expect. The digest and the reader are linear in the package's size, with a scaling test. Nothing loads a dependency yet.
