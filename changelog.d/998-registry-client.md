- **The registry client** (#998, ADR 0122 rules 26 and 34 to 40, `spec/30 §14`). `una ita lock`, `update` and `add` resolve the needs of a source of `sources` from its registry, read from the first of its places, the mirrors Itaoca's `mirrors.una` lists then its own URL, whose copy verifies, and verified from the pinned root to every entry: the root chain and its expiry, checkpoints with their witnesses, freshness and floors, the log's tiles and proofs, each name's whole chain against the name map, the owner's keys at each statement's position with revocations and recoveries in witness time, and rule 26 between releases. `una ita fetch` fills Itaoca with the locked releases, each archive checked against its statement, what its code uses included, and a verified release that a need names is mounted in the program. A change of an owner's keys needs `--accept-key` (the new code U7014). A mirror that serves a bad copy is skipped and named (U7010). This release has no https transport, so a source with no mirror directory, listed as a `file://` URL, is refused (U7013), and plain `http://` is never used.
