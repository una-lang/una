- **Limits on `Http` grants** (#970, ADR 0117 rule 8, IF4, `spec/27 §9`), in edition `0.4`. An `http` grant of `run grants` in `ita.una` takes limit lines, `at most <n> per second`, `minute` or `hour`, `at most <n> at once` and `waiting at most <n>` (1024 without it), and a `--grant` spec takes them as suffixes, `;100/minute;8/once;waiting=1000`; an invalid line is U7008. Both engines enforce them in the HTTP client they share, below every handler and rebinding: a token bucket per grant kept by GCRA on the run's clock, so that under `--virtual-time` a wait for a refill moves the virtual clock and both engines read the same times, a count of requests in flight, and a queue served by ticket. A request is charged to every grant that allows it, and each redirect hop to the grants of its target, releasing its slots before the next hop asks, so crossing redirect chains complete. A request over a limit waits, within `--http-timeout` and the deadlines of `within` and `for ever within`, and fails with the new `HttpError.RateLimited` when the queue is full; a canceled task's wait leaves the queue unsent. `una check` reports U4001 at each `Http` request timed by data above `internal` when `run grants` carry limits; `una build` embeds `L_lim`, and `una run` and executables refuse a limited `--grant` before `main` (exit code 2) when `L_lim` is above `internal` or the program is of an earlier edition.
