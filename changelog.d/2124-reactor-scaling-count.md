- **The reactor's scaling test counts its steps** (#2124, `crates/una-rt/src/reactor.rs`). The test that registering, waking and removing a wait are O(1) failed the gate `sanitize` on its wall-clock bound under AddressSanitizer. It now counts every access to the table's vectors, in the unit tests only (outside them the vectors are `Vec` itself), and bounds each `insert`, `take` and `remove` by a constant at 1,000 to 100,000 waits; the timing stays as a second check, one measurement taken again up to twice only when the bound is missed, and does not run under Miri or AddressSanitizer. The time bound is unchanged.
