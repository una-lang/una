- **The dense kernels in `una-core`** (#988, ADR 0120 rules 44 to 54, `spec/29 §17` rule 32). Sums, inner products, `norm2`, matrix products, LU, Cholesky and the triangular solves have blocked kernels beside their reference chains, with the same bits: products run by tiles of 64 columns and blocks of 64 terms, LU and Cholesky are right-looking with trailing updates by tiles, and a triangular solve runs every column of its right-hand side at once, each in O(1) space beyond its result. Their `fma` is the instruction on AArch64 and, on x86-64, an exact emulation by error-free transformations and rounding to odd, which falls back to the exact integer routine outside the range of its proof, so it is correctly rounded for every operand. Every stored float is canonical and no kernel exits early on a value. The interpreter runs the blocked kernels, reading contiguous operands in place. A corpus of inputs and the bits of their outputs, written by `scripts/dense-corpus.py` from an independent implementation with exact rational `fma`, is checked bit for bit against every kernel, and exact operation counts at growing sizes check the stated complexities.
