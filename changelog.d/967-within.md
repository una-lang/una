- **`within` bounds the time of an expression** (#967, ADR 0117 rules 4, 5 and 13, IF2, `spec/27 §5`), in edition `0.4`. `let quote = fetch_quote() within 2 * second` fails with `Timed[E]` of `std.time` (`TimedOut`, or `Failed(error: x)` for a failure in time), or `Deadline.Expired` when the expression cannot fail; `attempt f(x) within d` holds the time-out in its `Result`, and `if error pass` passes it as the function's own `TimedOut`. Past its deadline the expression is cancelled at its next check point, with the tasks it started, and its `Http` waits are cut; an operation in progress, its handler's code included, completes first, and the trace gains a `timeout` event after the operations' own outcomes. Checks: U2028 for a deadline that is not in milliseconds, U5001 for `within 0`, U3007 when the time-out is not handled, U3008 for `if error pass` without a field-less `TimedOut`, and the timing label of ADR 0117 IF2 (U4001, U4002, U4003), with each `within` above `public` in the report's `timing` list. Timers live in a timing wheel, O(1) to arm and cancel.
