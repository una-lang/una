- **Accepted: arenas and index-based graphs** (#82, ADR 0111). In edition `0.3`, `Arena[T]` will hold values under opaque `Id[T]` ids that are unique per run, printable never and comparable only by `==`; reading or writing an id needs a proof that the arena holds it, and an arena's structure carries its own label, so graphs and trees with parent links need no references. Accepted on a condition: no code relies on it until the formal model (F1) covers its frame facts.
