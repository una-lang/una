- **The serve timing oracles in both engines** (#1660, ADR 0123 S4 design §11.5, `spec/31 §6`). Each of the five release timing oracles of the serve threads, `serve_prefix_secret`, `serve_prefix_early_peers`, `serve_handoff_residue`, `serve_delivery_history` and `serve_owner_spread`, now runs once over the executable that `una build` makes (`<oracle>_native`) and once under `una run` (`<oracle>_interpreter`), with the same harness, assertions and sample sizes, since the serve engine's waiting and waking is separate code in each engine.
