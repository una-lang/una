- **Edition 0.5: collections and the numeric foundation move there from edition 0.4** (#1621, ADR 0139 rules 6 to 8, ADR 0119 and ADR 0120 amendments of 2026-11-06). Edition `0.5` is a new preview that `ita.una` may name: it has every construct of edition `0.4`, plus `std.collections`, `Map.update`, `Decimal[s]`, `F32`, the finite kinds, `Complex`, the dense types and constructors, `simd` as a name and the `with:` and `and:` labels. In edition `0.4` these are what edition `0.3` makes of them (U1001, U0101, U0103), since no release served them there (ADR 0139 rule 7), with the advice to write `edition 0.5`. A package of edition `0.4` checks the same in edition `0.5` but for those constructs, which a new edition test pins.
