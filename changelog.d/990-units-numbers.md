- **Units of measure on the new numbers** (#990, ADR 0114 rule 12, ADR 0120 rules 61 to 65, `spec/25 §9`). `of` follows `F64` from edition 0.3, and `F32`, `Decimal[s]`, `Complex[F64]` and `Complex[F32]` in edition 0.4, and a dense value carries its unit in its element type, as `Matrix[F64 of Meter]`. `+`, `-` and the comparisons need one unit, the comparisons of decimals across scales; `*`, `/` and the products (`dot`, `multiply`, `multiply_array`, `multiply_elements`, a scalar's `*`, `scale(by:)` of a complex value) multiply or divide units, and `sqrt()` and `norm_sqr()` halve and double the exponents; each method keeps, needs or refuses a unit by its rule, as `to_bits()` and `coefficient()` need a unitless receiver. `convert()` multiplies or divides an `F64` by a ratio of at most 2^53 and an `F32` by one of at most 2^24, and multiplies a decimal's coefficient by an integer ratio, exactly; a currency is a unit, and an exchange rate a value of a quotient unit. Mismatches are U2028 and refused conversions U2030. Units are erased before verification and code generation, so they cost nothing and change no value.
