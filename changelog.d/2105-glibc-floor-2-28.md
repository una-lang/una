- **Linux: `una` runs on glibc 2.28 and later** (#2105, ADR 0124 rule 1, `scripts/local-ci/Dockerfile`, `supply-chain/z3.lock`, `install.sh`). The Linux archive and the Z3 library inside `una` are now built in a release image of their own, `scripts/local-ci/Dockerfile.release`: AlmaLinux 8 (glibc 2.28, maintained until 2029), pinned by digest, with GCC 13 of gcc-toolset-13 (13.3.1) and binutils 2.40, where they were built in the Ubuntu 24.04 image of the tests (glibc 2.39, GCC 13.3.0). The tests, the formal track and the hunt stay in the Ubuntu image, since the pinned Lean toolchain needs glibc 2.29 and the Z3 project's Linux executable 2.38; `scripts/local-ci.sh --linux-arm64` runs them there and then packs and checks the archive in a second container of the release image, and `scripts/local-release.sh` builds the archive there. The floor was chosen by measurement among 2.35, 2.31 and 2.28: 2.28 is the oldest candidate whose distribution is still maintained and that has GCC 13, the compiler of the earlier recipe; the 2.31 distributions (Debian 11, Ubuntu 20.04) are past their standard support and have GCC 10 at most, and Ubuntu 22.04 (2.35) has GCC 12 at most. The `una` built there asks the loader for `GLIBC_2.28` at most, which `scripts/check-release.sh` checks on the packed `una` and on an executable it builds. So the archive of the next release installs and runs on glibc 2.28 and later, where 0.4.0 to 0.5.1 needed glibc 2.39: tested on Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 and AlmaLinux 8, and inferred from the symbol versions for Ubuntu 20.04, Debian 10 and 11, and RHEL 8 and later and their rebuilds. `install.sh` refuses a system below 2.28 for the releases after 0.5.1, and keeps the floor 2.39 for `UNA_VERSION` 0.4.0 to 0.5.1, whose `una` cannot start on an older glibc. What changes in the archive beyond the baseline: before glibc 2.34 threads and `dlsym` are libraries of their own, so `una` also names `libpthread.so.0` and `libdl.so.2`, glibc's, now listed in `scripts/load-commands.txt`. `libstdc++` and `libgcc` stay linked statically. The toolchain check reads the image's packages with `rpm -q` where it read `dpkg-query`, and the lock records the AlmaLinux packages and the digests of the files the linker puts into `una`; the new Z3 library digest was shown reproducible by cold builds in the new image. The release record and the public summary accept the version line of gcc-toolset's strip (`GNU strip version 2.40-21.el8`), in that exact form. ADR 0124 gains a dated amendment (Proposed) that names `libpthread.so.0` and `libdl.so.2` among glibc's libraries and the release image as the image of rule 1. `scripts/build-z3.sh` asks GNU ar and ranlib for their deterministic mode (`D`), which gcc-toolset's binutils does not default to; Apple's ar is unchanged, so the macOS library's digest is unchanged.
