- **Every excerpt in the examples' READMEs is checked** (#827). Each `text` block of `examples/*/README.md` names what it shows in its fence's info string, `una="ARGS"` for what a command prints in the package and `file="PATH"` for an excerpt of one of its files, and `crates/una-cli/tests/guide.rs` checks it, with trailing spaces removed and `...` as the only wildcard, a whole line; a block without a marker fails. The blocks that show what a change to a package reports are marked too, and checked next.
