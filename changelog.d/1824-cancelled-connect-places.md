- **A request canceled while it connects keeps its socket place until its socket is closed** (#1824, `spec/27 §2` rule 3). In an executable, a request parked on the reactor whose task was canceled while its connection was still being made, as the loser of a `first of` racing a host that drops SYNs is, gave its place among the sockets back at once, while the connect pool's thread kept the socket open until the request's deadline. Such requests could open more sockets than the cap of three quarters of the descriptor limit, so a `Files` operation of another task could fail with `EMFILE`. The connect pool's job now keeps the place until it has closed its socket. Requests that connect, and the interpreter, are unchanged.
