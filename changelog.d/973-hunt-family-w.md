- **The security hunt's family W, tasks and time** (#973, ADR 0117 S8). Generators for `within`, `first of`, `tasks` and `std.limit` over code whose work a secret decides, edition 0.4: accepted forms that keep every timing outcome at the secret's label, and closed calls that miss it, which a correct checker rejects. They are kept apart from the release gate's fixed run (seed 767, unchanged) and reached by `python3 -m hunt gen --ops W`, for the oracle under the cost clock.
