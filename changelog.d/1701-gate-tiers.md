- **Gate tiers: a fast layer per pull request and a nightly full suite** (#1701). The `tests` gate runs `scripts/gate-tests.sh` (the copy `origin/main` holds): a subset of the tests for documentation, corpus and test-only changes, the whole suite for every other change and for the security, soundness, performance and release workflows, and nightly on `main` (`docs/compiler/testing.md` section 8).
