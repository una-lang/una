- **Hardening: an overdue turn's end is raised again** (#1535, follow-up of S-43 / #1533, ADR 0117 rule 2): under the real clock the slicer raised the end of a task's turn once, so the Fairness bound rested on that one raise being heard. While a turn stays past its end with another task waiting on its worker, the slicer now raises it again once per further slice, so a raise that is lost delays the waiting task by a slice instead of for good. A task alone on its worker is never interrupted (edition `0.4` only; no released version has the scheduler).
