- **Itá project files: `ita.una`, `ita.lock` and `itaty.una`** (#893, ADR 0122, `spec/30`). A package of edition `0.4` describes itself in `ita.una`, read by Una's own lexer with a version token and text without interpolation, by a closed grammar; every problem is U7008 at its line, and every problem of `ita.lock` is U7009. The sections of `una.toml` have their `ita.una` form with the same checks. `needs`, `sources`, `replace` and `trust` are read and checked (reserved local names and their skeletons, owners bound to their source, no git origin), and each need is refused until dependencies load. `una ita lock [--check]` writes or checks the lock, the binary invoked as `ita` is `una ita`, and `una fmt ita.una` formats the file. The proof cache's key for edition `0.4` covers the lock's solver section only, under the domain tag `una-proof-cache/2`. Editions `0.1` to `0.3` do not change.
