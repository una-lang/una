- **Dense values in native code** (#989, ADR 0120 rules 26 to 43, `spec/29 §17`). `una build` compiles programs with `Array`, `Matrix`, `Tensor` and `Lu` values: one header per value over a store of raw canonical `F64` elements shared by its views and, across tasks, by the copies of a `together` block, or of words otherwise; every buffer reserved within the memory budget before it is allocated (U6003 at the call, never an abort of the process); `set`, `update`, `add_product`, `add_scaled` and `scale` in place when the value and its store are held once, and a copy of the value's own elements otherwise; the kernels of the interpreter, so both engines print the same text and bits.
