- **The documents of edition `0.5` write `const` and `var`** (#1630, ADR 0137 implementation plan step 3). The Una blocks of edition `0.5` in the specification, the collections of `spec/28` and the shape frame of `spec/29 §17` rule 18, were moved with `una fix` and now start with `-- edition 0.5`; the edition note of each chapter says so. The descriptions of `pop`, `pop_back` and `pop_ranked` in `una std` and `docs/std/builtins.json`, methods of edition `0.5` only, write `const x = edit s.pop()`, and the `una explain` texts of U2037 and U2039, codes of edition `0.5`, name `const`. A new document check fails when a `una` block of edition `0.5` in the specification, the guides, the release notes or the `una explain` texts writes `let`, except where it shows U7007, and when a block of a chapter of edition `0.5` does not say its edition. Editions `0.1` to `0.4` keep `let` and `let edit`: their conformance cases, examples and guide pages do not change, and no package of edition `0.5` in the repository still wrote `let`.
