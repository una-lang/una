- **A labeled `Json` names the constructor argument that labeled it** (#917, ADR 0073 J5, amendment of 2026-11-09 accepted on 2026-11-10). In edition 0.4, a U4001, U4002 or U4003 caused by a labeled `Json` value adds "the argument of `Str` made a `Json` value `secret`" to its message and shows that argument as a related location: the innermost, earliest labeled argument of a `Json` constructor in the declaration whose label the flow carries above what the sink allows.
