- **The debug runtime's memory budget no longer charges the slots its quarantine keeps** (#1506, ADR 0061). A debug `una-rt` reuses a freed slot only after 65,536 others were freed. Its slot table therefore grew about 5 MiB past a release build's, which made native tests with two such heaps under 16 MiB flaky with U6003. The budget now credits the freed slots past a heap's peak of live objects, within the table capacity grown since that peak, and at most a quarter of the budget. Live slots and capacity a release build also holds are never credited, and the hard ceiling of twice the budget stays on the bytes really held. Release builds are unchanged.
