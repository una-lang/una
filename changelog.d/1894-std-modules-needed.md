- **The optional standard modules are parsed only by the checks that take them** (#1894). `std.collections`, `std.limit` and the map module of edition 0.5 were parsed on every check of every edition and then given to the checker only when the package's edition and imports asked for them; now they are parsed only then, which no check of edition 0.1 to 0.3 does. The checker's result is unchanged: it received the same modules before. A test pins which modules each load parses. The cost of loading the standard modules is linear in the text parsed, a third less for editions 0.1 to 0.3. `una check` of a small edition 0.2 package retires 1.4 million fewer instructions of 48.5 million (2.9%); `una check examples/hello` 1.5 million of 40.0 million (3.7%).
