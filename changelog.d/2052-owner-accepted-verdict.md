- **The installer accepts a release that its record marks `ACCEPTED BY THE OWNER`** (#2052). A record committed for a tag may carry the verdict line `Verdict: ACCEPTED BY THE OWNER`, the owner's recorded decision for a release whose gates did not all pass on its commit; `install.sh` accepts it like `PASS (every gate)` and still checks the archive's digest against the record, which it reads from the tag's tree. v0.4.0 is released this way: its gates ran in full on its parent candidate (19 of 20 passed) and the `release` gate ran on its commit.
