- **Oracle for the placement of connections** (#1660, ADR 0123 S4 design §11.5, SC1). The release oracle `serve_owner_spread` observes directly, in the toolchain's test builds only, which serve thread placement chose for each of 8 connections over 8 serve threads whose source ports are all multiples of 8, in both engines and for both keys of its program. Under the keyed placement no run may put all 8 on one owner, and over the 160 connections of 20 runs every serve thread must own between 3 and 44, bounds fixed in advance from the binomial distribution; as a positive control, the unkeyed port-mod-8 placement must put all 8 on one owner in every run. Its timings are logged as diagnostics and are not asserted. It shows that the keyed placement spreads connections that the unkeyed port-mod-8 placement concentrates; it does not show that a peer cannot predict an owner, which is assumed from the secrecy of the run's key and SipHash-2-4, and it does not cover a fixed key or other unkeyed placements.
