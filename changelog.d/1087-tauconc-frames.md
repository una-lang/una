- **The timing labels of nested sites are computed in near-linear time** (#1087, ADR 0116 amendment of 2026-10-25, E, slice 8 of the precise `τ_conc`). Each item a site's checking records goes once into the innermost open site, every site's label is joined bottom up from the summaries, and the reads of outer bindings are resolved by one sweep per package, so sites nested `d` deep cost O(d log d) instead of O(d²). No verdict or message changes.
