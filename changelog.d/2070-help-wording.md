- **Wording of the command-line help, the installer and the `una ita verify` guide** (#2070, items 15, 20 and 39 of the 0.4.0 documentation review). The help of `--workers`, `--io-threads` and `--file-threads` of `una run` and `una test` says that they take effect in an executable of `una build` and where the interpreter, which runs its tasks on one thread, only checks them; the `--from` of `una ita add` and `una ita watch` says that the default `itaty` is not served by this release; `una ita` is the package manager of editions 0.4 and later, `una migrate` writes the later edition una.toml names, and `una run` also runs a `drive` or `serve` section. The installer no longer names 0.1.x, which it refuses earlier, when no validation summary can be read, and says what to check. The guide says why `una ita verify` reports a package whose copies match the lock as not verified (U7013): the registry client checks the key chain when it reads a release, and does not store it.
