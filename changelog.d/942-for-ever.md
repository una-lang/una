- **`diverges`, `for ever` and `on stop`, edition 0.4 preview** (#942, ADR 0116 rules 8 to 10, `spec/26 §5`). A function that may run forever says `diverges` in its header, which `una contract` reports; `for ever` and calls of such functions are legal only in its body, outside lambdas, `together` tasks, `on stop` blocks and `for ever` bodies (U3027), and such a function is not a value (U3028). `for ever` runs until `stop` or a failure; its `on stop` block runs once the loop has ended, after a failure with the state from before the loop, and must not fail (U3029). Core IR gains the loop source `ever` and the loop's `on_stop` block.
