- **Each dependency module is checked under its own package's edition** (#2260, ADR 0122 rule 49; a change of the edition `0.5` preview, for the heading "Edition `0.5` preview: changes that may need edits" of ADR 0121 rule 6; kind: U1001, U1002, U2002). A path or registry dependency of edition `0.4` under a root of edition `0.5` was read under its own edition but type-checked under the root's: a valid `0.4` package that declares `to_decimal(n: Int)` or `update` on a `Map` was refused with U1002, and one that uses `Array[Int]` or `F32`, names of edition `0.5` only, was accepted. Now every rule of the checker that depends on the edition, in name resolution, the type check and the messages, takes the edition of the module's own package, in `una check`, `una test`, `una run`, `una build`, `una report` and every other command that checks, so a dependency gives what it gives when it is checked alone: no U1002 at those declarations, U2002 at `Array` and U1001 at `F32`. A rule of edition `0.5` at a use is the using module's: a root of edition `0.5` that imports such a `to_decimal` or `update` from a dependency of edition `0.4` is U1002 at the import, since the built-in resolves ahead of it there. The words `let` or `const` in a diagnostic are now chosen for each text by its own file, so a related note in a dependency of edition `0.4` keeps the words of `0.4` under a root of edition `0.5`. A root of edition `0.4` still cannot depend on a package of edition `0.5` (U7012). Packages without dependencies do not change. The cost is one lookup of the module's edition per rule, O(1).
