- **Security: the registry service bounds the work of a slow head and the memory of a declared body** (#1473). `una-itaty` searched a request's head for its end from the start after every read, so a peer that sent one byte at a time cost quadratic work; the search now resumes where it stopped. A body's buffer reserved its whole declared length (up to about 260 MiB for a publication) from the head alone, and a publication took its publishing slot only after its body was read, so many connections could exhaust memory with headers; the buffer now grows with the bytes that arrive, up to the declared length, and a publication takes its slot once its head is checked and before its body is read, so a third concurrent publication is refused with 503 before its body. The other limits, the strict parsing and the refusals are unchanged.
