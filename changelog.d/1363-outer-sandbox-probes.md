- **Security: the outer-sandbox check of the solver asks about the loopback network, local sockets and every write at fixed places** (#1363, #1364, #1365, S-31, S-32, ADR 0124 rule 7 and its amendment of 2026-11-01, `spec/13 §6`): on macOS, a solver child under an outer sandbox now takes it as its confinement only if it denies `network-outbound`, `network-inbound` and `network-bind` with no filter, toward `127.0.0.1` and `::1` over TCP and UDP and toward local sockets, and ten write operations (data, create, unlink, extended attributes, mode, flags, times, owner, ACLs and Finder info) at `/`, `/Users`, `/private/var`, `/private/tmp`, `/Volumes`, the account's home directory and the user's temporary directory, none taken from the working directory; before, a profile that allowed connections to `localhost`, writes under the home directory, or ACL, Finder-info, attribute and mode changes was accepted. An answer the library gives for an operation it does not know is U5003, never a denial. Once the outer sandbox is accepted, the child's open-file limit is zero, so no socket or file can be opened. `tcb.solver_outer_denied` names every probe, each denial being the outer sandbox's (profile or system). Signals to other processes and writes that need no descriptor outside those places are accepted residuals the outer profile must deny; `scripts/toolchain-free.sh` and the `hunt` gate deny them. The outer-sandbox tests fail, instead of passing, under a sandbox that refuses a nested one unless `UNA_SKIP_NESTED_SANDBOX_TESTS=1` is set, a failure to put stderr back after `sandbox_init` still reports its reason, and `crates/una-sys/tests/solver_size.rs` caps the confinement files' size.
