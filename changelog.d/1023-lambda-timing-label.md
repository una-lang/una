- **A lambda written at the call counts by its captures in the timing label** (#1023, ADR 0116 IF5 item 4, `spec/26 §2` rule 5 and §7 rule 5). A call of `map`, `filter`, `first_where`, `fold`, the dense `map`, `zip_map`, `update` and `fold`, or the `update` of a map, whose every function argument is a lambda written at the call no longer gives a driven step, a `within`, a `first of`, a `for ever within` or a `limiter` the top timing label: the lambda's captures and what its body runs count as code written outside it does. Any other function value, a parameter, a local, a field or a named function, keeps the top label, as do `L_res` and `L_lim`. The rule only makes the label less strict, and a test keeps its premise that no standard function keeps a function argument.
