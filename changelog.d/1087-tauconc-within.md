- **A `within` takes the label of the work that can run beside it** (#1087, ADR 0116 amendment of 2026-10-25, P0 to P5, slice 3 of the precise `τ_conc`, new `spec/27 §11`). The timing label of a `within` that a part of a task construct can reach is now `κ` of the constructs around it, the timing label of their parts, instead of `⊤`: a deadline beside started tasks or branches that handle only `public` data stays `public`, and one beside work on a `secret` is `secret`. A `within` in a lambda or in a handler's operation that no part reaches is now `public` (P5), where the preview gave it `⊤`. The other timing sites keep the preview's `⊤` until their slices.
