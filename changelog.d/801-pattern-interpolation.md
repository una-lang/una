- **A text literal with interpolation in a pattern is U4008, in every edition** (#801, S-28). A `when` arm `is "…"` and the expression `x is "…"` treated their literal as a constant, `public`, but a text literal with interpolation is computed from the values it interpolates. So a `when` could choose the arm that prints, and an `is` give a `public` `Bool`, by a `secret` value, with no diagnostic. A pattern's literal is now a constant: interpolating in it is U4008, whose explanation shows the repair, comparing with `==`, whose result carries the value's label. Every release up to and including v0.3.14 and v0.1.10 accepts such programs (those two confirmed by running): the parser and the checker have read a pattern's text this way since v0.1.0. This is a soundness fix, so it applies to editions 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 alike, and a program it now rejects was leaking.
