- **`overflow` obligations on `Int` in `systems` packages** (#31, ADR 0109 rule 3). In a package whose profile is `systems`, each `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `mod` and unary `-` on `Int` (and on `Int of U`) outside clauses, `convert()` on an `Int` magnitude, calls of `sum` and `abs`, and `to_int()` on `I64` and `U64` carry an `overflow` obligation that the exact result lies in -2^62 to 2^62 - 1, and an `Int` literal outside that range is refuted at once (U5001); the repairs are a guard, a refinement, a fixed-width type, or a wrapping or saturating method of one. The Core program records the profile, and the Core export's header reads it from there. `app` packages are unchanged.
