- **`together for` placement: the spec and the message name its three forms** (#929, `spec/19 §1` rule 2). A `together for` may be the value of a `let`, the value of an assignment or the last line of a block; `spec/19` now says so, and from edition `0.2` the U0101 message for a misplaced `together for`, `when`, `if` with blocks or `first of` names assignments too. No change to what parses.
