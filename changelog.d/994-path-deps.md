- **Path dependencies** (#994, ADR 0122 rules 7, 42, 47 to 52, `spec/30 §12`). A package of edition `0.4` may need a package from a local directory, `util from path "../util"` with `accept name`, and import its modules under its local name, `import util.text (shout)`. The dependency is checked with the root as one program, read with no symbolic link followed, and every module of it is a package extent with the ceiling the root writes in `may` lines: a capability reaches it only by `with`, within that ceiling (U3020, U3021). `una ita lock` locks it with its digest and the ceiling granted, and records a wider ceiling only with `--accept-authority` (U7009). New codes: U1010 (a module named like a dependency's local name) and U7012 (a dependency the root may not use as written). Registry and workspace sources, and dependencies with needs, labels, trusted modules, boundaries or contracts of their own, are refused until their slices land.
