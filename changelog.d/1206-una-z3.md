- **The crate `una-z3`, and the development-build line** (#1206, ADR 0124 rules 2, 19, 20 and 35, the second slice of step 1): `una-z3` holds the call into the Z3 front end built by `scripts/build-z3.sh`, linked by its build script from `UNA_Z3_LIB` (development) or `UNA_EMBED_Z3` with its checked digest (release), and runs it only in a process that recorded its confinement, which `una-sys` keeps behind the feature `solver`; `una --version` ends with `development build`, and its document gains the key `build`, in a build without the release solver; `una` carries a build record with the solver's pin and library digest; ADR 0058 gains the amendment of ADR 0124.
