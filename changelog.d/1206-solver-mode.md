- **The solver inside `una`** (#1206, ADR 0124 rules 3 to 8, 10 and 11, `spec/12 §5`, the fourth slice of step 1): `una check` runs each query in `una` itself, re-executed as `una __solver`, which confines itself (descriptors, limits, the seccomp filter on Linux or the sandbox profile on macOS), writes the handshake `una-solver 1 z3 <version> <build>` and runs the Z3 front end built into `una`; no `z3` on `PATH` is ever run, and the lock's pin is compared with the built-in version with no process started. A proof needs the handshake, a normal exit with status 0 and `unsat`; a child past its processor time, past its memory, or crashed is `unknown` with reason `cpu`, `memout` or `crash`, never cached; a child killed by its confinement is U5003. `scripts/local-ci.sh` builds the Z3 library of `supply-chain/z3.lock` once and names it by `UNA_Z3_LIB`; the toolchain's own tests use a stand-in solver child under the feature `test-hooks`.
