- **Proposed: floating point, `F64`** (#819, ADR 0114, Proposed). Edition `0.3` would gain `F64`, IEEE 754 binary64, with literals such as `0.5` and `6.674_30e-11`, correctly rounded arithmetic and `sqrt`, and explicit conversions only. Results would be bitwise identical in both engines and on every target: one canonical NaN, no fused or reassociated operations, subnormals kept, and no platform math library. `==` would compare bits, so `nan() == nan()` and `-0.0 != 0.0`, and `<` would be IEEE totalOrder, so floats work as map keys and in `sort`; the IEEE comparisons are named methods (`ieee_lt` and the others). Floats would print by the shortest text that reads back to the same bits, and parse correctly rounded. Contracts over floats would be verified exactly, through the solver's floating-point theory, and programs without floats keep byte-identical queries. Units apply (`F64 of Meter`). Transcendental functions and `F32` are later steps.
