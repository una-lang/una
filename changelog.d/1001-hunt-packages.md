- **The security hunt covers programs of several packages** (#1001, ADR 0122). Family D tries authority, information flow and trust across a dependency's boundary, from a path or from a vendored registry release; family Y tries registry packages, with a registry, provenance and locks the hunt builds itself from `spec/30`: forged vendored copies, provenance and locks, which must be refused, and minimal version selection with yanks, avoided releases, pre-releases and the cooldown, judged by `una ita lock --check`. The hunt gate runs 6,788 programs, with three new canaries.
