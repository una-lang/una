- **`result`, `it` and `error` inside a lambda are U2012 and U2013** (#1989, `spec/02 §8`). A lambda's body is not a position of `result`, `it` or `error`, even when the lambda is written in an `expect` line, a `where` clause, the predicate of `checked` or an `if error` handler: the checker reports U2012 for `result` and U2013 for `it` and `error` at the use, where v0.4.0 and v0.5.0 rejected the package with the internal error U8001. A value the body needs is passed as an argument. The guide's new §23 lists the change. Elaboration also fails closed, with U8001, at three more places where a well-typed program never reaches: a call in a clause whose arguments leave a parameter without one, a condition written on a `uses` clause that no capability entry carries, and a name, constructor, call or `when` arm that resolved to something its syntax cannot elaborate to. An `if error` handler restores the binder of an enclosing one instead of clearing it.
