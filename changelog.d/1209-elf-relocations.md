- **ELF relocation engine for Linux, step 4b of the internal linker** (#1209, ADR 0124 rules 24 and 33). `una-link` gains a reader of ELF relocatable objects for `x86_64-unknown-linux-musl` and `aarch64-unknown-linux-musl` and the relocation engine of rule 24: every supported type of both targets, the x86-64 GOT relaxations, the thread-local relaxations to the local-exec model (general- and local-dynamic through `PLT32` or the GOT, initial-exec, arm64 descriptors), relative fixups for 64-bit pointers in writable sections, and the refusals of rule 24 (narrow absolute and text relocations, PC-relative references a position-independent executable cannot make, places outside their section or overlapping, values that do not fit, branches beyond their reach as U7015), with bounded reads of any input. The arm64 types no input uses and `ld.lld` does not apply (the checked `TLSLE_*_TPREL_LO12` forms and the tiny-model `TLSDESC_LD_PREL19`/`ADR_PREL21`) are left out of the list, which the musl scan now shares. Each type is checked byte for byte against `ld.lld` in `cargo test` on macOS, and against GNU `ld` by `scripts/link-oracle-elf.sh`; every relocation of the toolchain's musl inputs and standard library is applied in a test; a new fuzz target, `elf_link`. Nothing is linked with it yet: `una build` on Linux still links through `cc` until the layout of the next slice.
