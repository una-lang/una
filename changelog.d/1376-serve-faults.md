- **Serve thread faults** (#1376, after #1375, ADR 0123 step 4, `spec/31 §6` rule 13), edition 0.4 preview. A panic in a connection's step, or in a callback registered on the serve threads, is caught and counted: it closes that connection at most and never ends a serve thread. A serve thread that ends by any other panic closes every connection it owned and gives back their `connections` slots, peers' places, requests, exchanges and replies, hands the peers' expiries it was timing to a live thread (so `https` peer entries still expire) and hands on the turn to accept; a message sent to it later is handled by its sender, and a new connection whose keyed owner ended goes to the next live serve thread, or is refused at accept when none is left. A connection whose answer timer cannot be armed no longer leaves a dead entry in a delivery queue. A second registration of `on_ready` or `on_slot_freed` is now the defined error `AlreadyRegistered` in every build instead of stopping a debug build. The turn to accept is recorded before the message that passes it, so the record is never older than the last pass. No program can name `HttpServer` yet (#1007).
