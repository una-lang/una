- **Una Quality, proposed** (#1296, ADR 0127 Proposed, `spec/32` Draft): the foundation record of `una quality`, a deterministic report of measured quality with three gates (assurance, structural, evolution) that never compensate each other; `quality.toml` with a severity (`off`, `info`, `warn`, `block`) for every metric, presets, waivers and baselines; a status on every value; codes `UQ` plus four digits. The draft chapter defines every metric, the finding record, the documents, the exit codes and the first `UQ` codes with their explain texts. Nothing is implemented.
