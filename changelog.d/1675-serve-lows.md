- **Serve engine: an undeliverable request no longer blocks its queue, and `Host` refuses a last label of `0x`** (#1675, ADR 0123 rules 13 and 14, `spec/31 §3` rule 5 and `§6` rule 5). A request whose bytes exceed the whole of the grant's `delivered` is answered `503` with `Retry-After: 1` at once, when its head is read for a declared `Content-Length` and when it is read in full for a chunked body, instead of holding the front of its serve thread's queue until `answer`, so the requests behind it are delivered; a last label of exactly `0x` or `0X` is refused in `Host` (`400`) and in a grant's `hosts`, as one that starts with `0x` already was.
