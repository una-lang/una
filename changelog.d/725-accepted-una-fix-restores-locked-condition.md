- **Accepted, not yet implemented: `una fix` restores a locked condition** (#725, ADR 0092 and ADR 0084, amendments of 2026-10-15). When a locked public function's header is weaker than its `[contracts]` entry, the first repair of the refuted `contract_lock` obligation (U3023) will carry an edit that writes the locked condition back on the header's operation, `op where C_lock` or `op where (C_src) and (C_lock)`, and `una fix` will apply it in its tier-1 rounds. The edit only adds a condition, never changes `una.toml`, and is withheld while any obligation is unknown. Nothing is implemented yet.
