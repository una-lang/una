- **Labels on `Clock` readings, proposed** (#898, ADR 0116 Proposed amendment of 2026-11-09, `spec/14 §3`). In edition 0.4, a reading of `Clock.now` would carry the timing label of the code that ran before it, by the ancestors' rule of `L_lim`, so that elapsed time over `secret` data is no longer a `public` value. The amendment states what it breaks and how programs that only log or measure time keep working; nothing is implemented yet.
