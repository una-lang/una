- **Proposed: charging the copies of a `together` block to the memory budget** (#777, ADR 0113, Proposed). The values a block gives its tasks, and their results, would be charged to the budget by one model size, the same in both engines, and checked once at the block's fork and once at its join, on the block's own task, instead of on each task's thread while it copies. A run over its budget at a block would stop there with U6003 in both engines and on every platform, and the native case of #314, whose site varied with how threads were scheduled, would stop at one site. `una run` would charge these copies too, so it can report U6003 at a block where today it runs on.
