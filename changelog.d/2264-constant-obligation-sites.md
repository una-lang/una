- **Module constants: no pending `overflow` or `division` site inside a constant's value** (#1632, ADR 0126 rules 5 and 6). A constant's value is evaluated at check time, where a fixed-width value that does not fit and a division by zero are U2041, and Core IR holds the folded literal, so the checker no longer lists such a site as a pending obligation that the verifier never generates (the workspace test that compares the two sites failed on `main` after #2261).
