- **`una --version` names the preview editions, and the report names the edition** (#1621, ADR 0121 rules 7 and 8). `una --version` prints `preview: 0.3, 0.4, 0.5` after `editions:`, and `una.version/1` gains `"preview"` after `"editions"`, `[]` when there is none. `una.report/2` gains `edition`, after `package`, with the package's edition and whether it is a preview, and the human report prints `edition: 0.5 (preview)`. Both keys are additive. A test checks that the previews of `una --version` are the editions that `spec/13 §7` marks as previews.
