- **`una report` lists the conditions of ADR 0092 in `authority` and `handler_authority`** (#864, `spec/13 §6`): each `uses` entry is printed as the header writes it, so an operation keeps its condition and its label, `op gives label where C`, in the human output and in `--json`, as `una contract` shows them. A header with `uses @printer: Printer[print_label where not dry_run]` was reported as `@printer: Printer[print_label]`, more authority than it grants, and a header whose condition was deleted gave the same report as one that kept it. An operation without a condition or a label prints as before, so the reports of edition `0.1` and `0.2` packages do not change; the entries stay strings and the schema stays `una.report/2`.
