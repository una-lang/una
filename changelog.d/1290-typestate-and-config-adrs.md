- **Protocols and configuration files, proposed** (#1290, #1302, ADR 0130 and ADR 0131 Proposed): ADR 0130 lets a capability declare facts about the resources behind it, which its operations require, establish and invalidate, a failure making them unknown; the verifier tracks them step by step, across calls through frames in headers and across loop iterations, inside QF_UFLIA, with no run-time cost; B04's violation fails at build, and B05's batch form is left to a record on facts over list elements. ADR 0131 makes every configuration file a person writes for Una a `.una` file in Una's lexical syntax with a closed grammar, told from a module by its place and its first word, read by the one syntax crate; `quality.una` replaces `quality.toml` when its slice lands, through `una migrate`, and the interim TOML reader is accepted until then (with a dated amendment of ADR 0122). Nothing is implemented.
