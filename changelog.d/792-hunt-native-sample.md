- **The `hunt` release gate compares the native backend with the interpreter on a sample of its programs** (#792). About 5% of the oracle's programs, a fixed sample, are built with `una build` and run natively, every variant. Their exit code, output, failure and trace must equal the interpreter's (`spec/16 §5`), by the rules of the native equivalence test, the ones for tasks and resource aborts included. A difference is a backend divergence and fails the gate. A sampled program the native backend does not compile must be pinned by name, and a canary checks that the comparison reports a planted divergence.
