- **Edition `0.4` opens as a preview** (#888, ADR 0116 rule 0, `spec/26 §1`). `una.toml` accepts `edition = "0.4"`, which has every construct, keyword and rule of edition `0.3`. It is the edition of long-running programs: `diverges` and `within` are reserved in it (U0103), and the constructs of ADR 0116 join it as they land. Earlier editions do not change, and `una --version` lists `0.4`.
