- **Una's own big integer** (#1093, epic #1097). `Int`, the coefficients of `Decimal` and the exact steps of floating-point conversions now use `una_core::bigint`, safe Rust with no dependency, in the interpreter and the native runtime; num-bigint, num-integer and num-traits leave the build graph of every crate. Every result is the same bit for bit, checked against num-bigint on random operands before the switch. Multiplication is schoolbook below 40 limbs, Karatsuba, and Toom-3 from 256 limbs; division is Knuth's algorithm D below 64 limbs of divisor or quotient and Burnikel-Ziegler above; text conversion divides and conquers past 32 limbs; a value of one limb never allocates. The 4,300-digit bound on writing and parsing an integer as text stays as specified (#1223 lifts it). `scripts/no-libm.sh` now asserts that the release graph holds none of those crates.
