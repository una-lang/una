- **The formal re-check refuses the collections of `std.collections` by name** (#983, ADR 0119 implementation plan step 8, `spec/28 §12`). F1 does not model `Stack`, `Queue`, `Set`, `HashMap`, `HashSet`, `PriorityQueue` or `BitSet` yet, so the re-check reports a declaration whose Core holds one of their types, methods or functions, and what reaches it, as not re-checked, never accepted, with the reason "F1 restriction (the collection ..., ADR 0119)". Such declarations were already not re-checked, as forms the importer did not read; the reason now names the collection, and `formal/UnaFormal/F1/CollectionsImportTests.lean` pins the verdicts on `una`'s own export.
