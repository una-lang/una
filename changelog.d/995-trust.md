- **Trusted modules of dependencies** (#995, ADR 0122 rules 10, 53 and 53.1, `spec/30 §13`). A path dependency's trusted modules are trusted only by a trust line of the root naming each of them, and by the lock's consent to the package's whole content, which `una ita lock --accept-trust <package>` records again after any change (U7012 otherwise). Each is an extent of its own, entered with `with`, and a release whose policy it declares is not a release into a boundary. The report lists them under the dependency's `trust` and `tcb.dependency_trust`.
