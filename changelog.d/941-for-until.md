- **`for until c decreases m`, edition 0.4 preview** (#941, ADR 0116 rule 7, `spec/26 §4`). The loop ends at the start of an iteration where `c` holds; `m`, an integer in the refinement language that is never run, must stay at least 0 where the loop continues (`decreases_bound`) and decrease in every iteration that goes on (`decreases_step`), so the verifier proves that the loop ends. `not c` holds in the body, and `c` after a loop without `stop`. The body runs under the label of `c`. Without `decreases` the loop is U0101. Core IR gains the loop source `until`.
