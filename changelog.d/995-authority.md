- **Effective ceilings, `una ita tree` and `una ita remove`** (#995, ADR 0122 rules 51, 57 and 58, `spec/30 §12`). The report gains `dependencies`, one entry per package of the program other than the root, with its source, digest, the ceiling each dependent writes, its effective ceiling (rule 51's fixpoint) and the authority its code uses; `tcb` gains `registries` and `dependency_trust`. `una ita tree` prints the same per package, and `una ita remove <name>` takes a need out of `ita.una` and writes the lock. Neither opens a connection.
