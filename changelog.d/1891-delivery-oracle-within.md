- **The timing oracle `serve_delivery_history` waits with a `within`** (#1891, #898, #1660). Its positive control waited on a loop that stopped on `Clock.now`, which a program that performs an operation of `HttpServer` reads at `L_srv` in edition 0.4, so `una check` refused it (U4002); the wait is now a loop that never ends by itself under a `within` whose deadline the key decides, 1 ms or 1.5 s, the same channel. The conformance case `serve_delivery_history_secret` follows it, still refused with U4003, and the cases `clock_wait_server` (U4002) and `clock_wait_server_within` (accepted) record the two forms.
