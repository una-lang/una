- **The standard library reference: JSON and `KeyError`** (#853). `docs/std/reference.json` now describes `Json`, `JsonNumber`, `is_json_number`, `parse_json`, `JsonError`, `to_json` and `quote_json`, and `KeyError`, each with a checked example. `parse_json` states each failure as `spec/23 §3` gives it: the first error in reading order, a depth of at most 512, integers of at most 4300 digits, and no size limit beyond the memory budget. The costs of parsing and writing come from `spec/23 §6`. The examples' manifests are checked to be minimal.
