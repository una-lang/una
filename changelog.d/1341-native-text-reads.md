- **Native text reads no longer copy their operands** (#1341, ADR 0132 rules 5 and 10, `spec/22 §2`). In native code, `starts_with`, `ends_with`, `contains` on texts, `==` and `<` on texts, structural equality of a text leaf, and the order of `Text` in `contains`, `sort`, `min` and `max` now read the texts in place, borrowed from the heap, so a call costs what it reads rather than the sizes of its operands: `ends_with` is O(m), a comparison stops at the first difference, and `contains` of a long item over many short texts is linear in the size of the list. Results are unchanged; a conformance case covers empty texts, a prefix longer than the text, equal prefixes, a shared first byte and multi-byte scalar values, and two scaling tests hold the bounds.
