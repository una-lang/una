- **Security (S-45): a run's trace renders each operation's outcome at its label** (#1552, `spec/11 §5`, `spec/26 §6` rule 4, ADR 0032 amendment of 2026-11-06). The trace redacted an operation's `ok` value by its result's label but wrote whether it failed, its variant and its payload in clear, though the checker labels those decisions (ADR 0085, ADR 0094, `spec/27 §10`). So the outcomes of public `Files.read`s spelled out what hidden writes under an `internal` branch did, or the `internal` path of a public write, and a public `Limit.acquire` showed whether hidden code took the token, in both engines and every edition. In a run, an outcome whose label, the decision's joined with its failure type's and its result's, is above `public` is now `{"kind":"redacted","level":"internal"}`, with no text built from the value, in both engines and every edition; Core IR, `--dump-core`, `una --json core` (key `outcome`, additive in `una.core/1`) and the native description carry the label. Nothing stops compiling. The cost is observability: in a run's trace the outcomes of every `Http` request, `Files.read`, `Env.get`, `Keys.hmac`, `Limit.acquire`, `gives`-labeled operation and `Files` operation of a package whose file-state label is above `public` are redacted. An example's trace keeps its outcomes in clear. Readers of trace outcomes must accept the kind `redacted`.
