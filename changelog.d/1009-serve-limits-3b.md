- **Limits and timers of a `serve:` grant run alike in both engines** (#1009, ADR 0123 rules 7, 9, 12, 13 and 21, `spec/31 §2`, `§6`): scripted runs of `waiting` full and `/once` holding delivery, a rate delaying it, bodies over `buffers` and `peer_buffers` answered `503` at once, IPv6 peers counted per /64, and the `idle`, `read` and `write` timers give the same bytes, output and trace in the interpreter and in native code.
