- **`F32` and the finite kinds in edition 0.4** (#984, ADR 0120 rules 5 to 11 and V1, `spec/29 §18`). `F32` is IEEE binary32 under every rule of `F64`: one canonical NaN (`0x7FC00000`), `==` by identity, `<` by totalOrder, no contraction. A float literal is an `F32` where an `F32` is expected, an operator's other operand included, and is rounded once to binary32, never through binary64 (`0.1` is `0x3DCCCCCD`); a literal beyond the binary32 bounds is U2031. `F32` and `F64` do not mix (U2001, with a repair that converts); `to_f32()` converts an integer or an `F64`, `to_f64()` an `F32`, and `fma` rounds once in binary32. The prelude gains `parse_f32`, `f32_from_bits`, and the refined kinds `Finite64` and `Finite32`. The verifier encodes `F32` exactly as SMT-LIB `Float32`, both engines compute the same bits, native code holds an `F32` in one word without allocating, and the environment's self-test gains binary32 probes. `simd` is no longer reserved in edition 0.4. Edition 0.4 makes a declaration named `F32`, `Complex`, `Array`, `Matrix`, `Tensor` or `Lu`, and a package function `to_f32` on an integer, an `F64` or a type parameter, U1002.
