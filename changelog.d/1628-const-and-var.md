- **`const` and `var` replace `let` and `let edit` in edition `0.5`** (#1628, ADR 0137; a change of the edition `0.5` preview, for the heading "Edition `0.5` preview: changes that may need edits" of ADR 0121 rule 6; kind: U7007). In a package of edition `0.5`, a binding is written `const x = e`, where edition `0.4` writes `let x = e`, and `var x = e`, where it writes `let edit x = e`, with the same typing, Core IR, obligations, information flow and run-time behavior in both engines. `const` and `var` are keywords there, and `let` is reserved: each `let` is now U7007, "this form of edition 0.5 changed in release v0.5.2: `let` is written `const`, or `var` for a binding that is reassigned", at the word, whose first repair is the exact rewrite (`let edit x` to `var x`, `let x` to `const x`). Migration: `una fix` applies the rewrites. `const edit x`, `var edit x` and a `var` at the top level of a module are U0101, and `const` and `var` cannot name anything in edition `0.5`. Messages of a package of edition `0.5` name its binding words: U2019 says "declare it `var x`", U2024 "holds only `const` or `var` statements", and U2011 and U3013 "bind its result with `const` first". U7007, the code of a form a preview edition changed (ADR 0121 rule 19), is registered, with its `una explain` text; U2024's registry meaning names a binding statement. The edition ledger gains `const and var` (ADR 0137), and `una report` counts its sites. A dependency is lexed, parsed and formatted under its own edition (ADR 0122 rules 48 and 49), so a dependency of edition `0.4` of a package of edition `0.5` keeps `let` and its diagnostics keep the words of edition `0.4`; before, every mounted dependency was read with the root's keywords. Editions `0.1` to `0.4` do not change: there a binding written `const x` or `var x` keeps its U0101 or U0103 and gains the help "`const` and `var` are keywords of edition 0.5". The conformance packages of edition `0.5` are rewritten with the new words, and the comparison of every edition `0.4` package with edition `0.5` now rewrites its `let`s first. Module constants (ADR 0126, #1632), the `una fix` tests of ADR 0137 (#1629) and the migration of the repository's other Una code (#1630, #1631) follow. Lexing `const`, `var` and `let` is O(1) per word, a lookup in the edition's table; the rewrite of a message is O(its length).
