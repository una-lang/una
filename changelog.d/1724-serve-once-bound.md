- **The `serve` section's tasks are bounded by the grants' `/once`** (#1724, `spec/31 §7` rule 4, decision S6-D6). The driver's `tasks` statement had only the default bound, so a peer that left, or an `answer` that passed, gave back its exchange's `/once` place while `handle` kept running: with `at most 2 at once`, ten requests closed early left ten `handle` tasks running at once. The statement is now bounded by the sum of `/once` over the run's `serve:` grants, within the default bound of a `tasks` statement, in both engines, so a place is given back when `handle`'s task ends; `spec/31 §7` rule 4 says so.
