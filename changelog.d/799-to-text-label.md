- **The Core export labels values computed from a labeled value with that value's label** (#799, S-27). In edition 0.2 and later, interpolating a value writes its fields without redaction, and the checker labels the text with every label of the value's type, its fields' included; the export bound that text to a temporary labeled without the labels that kinds' fields declare, so a record with a `secret` field was exported as `public` text holding the field in clear. A `when` over a literal or a range compares the scrutinee, and the export labeled those comparisons `public` whatever the scrutinee's label. The formal re-check rejected such programs, rightly. The export now carries the checker's labels. No program's verdict, output or trace changes: the checker, the redaction of traces and the arguments of operations were already right.
