- **The Core export labels an interpolated value's text with its fields' labels** (#799, S-27). In edition 0.2 and later, interpolating a value writes its fields without redaction, and the checker labels the text with every label of the value's type, its fields' included. The Core export bound that text to a temporary labeled without the labels that kinds' fields declare, so a record with a `secret` field was exported as `public` text holding the field in clear. The formal re-check rejected such programs, rightly. The temporary now carries the checker's label. No program's verdict, output or trace changes: the checker, the redaction of traces and the arguments of operations were already right.
