- **The check of released changelog sections reads a record of amended sections, and two scaling tests measure processor time** (#2111). `scripts/check-released-sections.sh` failed on every commit since the neutral rewording of the released sections `## v0.3.4` and `## v0.3.8`, since it had no way to record a deliberate amendment. `scripts/released-sections-amended.txt` now holds one line per amendment: the tag, the SHA-256 of the amended section and the card. A section that differs from its tag passes only when its digest is the recorded one, and is named `amended`; an amended section edited further, a record whose section is identical to its tag, a line out of shape, two lines for one tag and a tag the check does not read all fail, and a section that differs is printed with its digest. The tags and their published release notes keep the original text. The tests `renaming_underscored_names_takes_linear_cpu_time` and `renaming_underscored_names_in_one_declaration_takes_linear_cpu_time` (`crates/una-cli/tests/recovery.rs`, before `..._scales_linearly`) read the processor time of a child of the test binary past its start, not the elapsed time, which read 2.9 and 2.6 times for a doubling inside the parallel test run of a release gate; the sizes and the bound of 2.5 are unchanged.
