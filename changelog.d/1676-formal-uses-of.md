- **The formal re-check reads a call of a function with `uses of` or `fails of` per call** (#1676, ADR 0062). F1 has no effect polymorphism and reads such a function as one that performs an operation and may fail, so a caller that gives it functions with no `uses` or no `fails` was rejected although its call performs nothing or cannot fail. When that reading is the only reason for a rejection, the caller is now reported as not re-checked, a form F1 does not model; any other operation, failure or flow of the caller is still rejected. No declaration becomes accepted.
