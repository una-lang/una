- **`Stack` and `Queue` in edition `0.4`** (#976, ADR 0119 rules 5 and 6). `std.collections` exports `empty_stack()` and `empty_queue()`. A stack has `edit s.push(item:)`, `edit s.pop()`, `peek()`, `items()` and `.len`; a queue has the same, with `push` at the back and `pop` at the front, and `edit q.push_front(item:)`, `edit q.pop_back()` and `peek_back()` at the other ends. A removal gives `Option[T]`, `None` on an empty collection. Updates are O(1) amortized and in place when no other binding holds the collection, and the buffer shrinks as it empties. Both compare with `==` and write as a list, top or front first. Labels follow a list's: an update under a `secret` condition into a `public` collection is U4001. The verifier knows their lengths and the refinement of a pushed item; the native backend reports both types as not yet supported. `spec/28` gains their section.
