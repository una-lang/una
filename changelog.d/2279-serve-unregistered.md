- **A serve connection whose readiness registration fails closes at once** (#2279, `spec/31 §6` rules 3 and 13). When `kqueue` or `epoll` refused to register what a connection waited for, the failure was dropped, and the connection, registered nowhere, waited with its reply pending until its `write` timer (30 s by default) cut it. The serve thread now closes such a connection at once and gives back what it held, as for a contained fault in its step; a registration interrupted by a signal is retried, up to three times. The `write` timer is still a total for the whole reply and is never extended by progress (`spec/31 §6` rule 3, `§2` rule 2). With the `test-hooks` feature of the toolchain's tests, the record of a reply cut short (#2273) also names this cause (`Unregistered`), records a victim of reply pressure and a reply larger than `replies` as `Discarded` even when their close comes before any write readiness, and counts the bytes of a host answer (`100 Continue`, a refusal) not written. O(1) per registration, as before.
