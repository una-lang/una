- **Security: `--stop-after-iterations` and `stop after N` are a budget of each loop instance** (#1472, S-36, `spec/26 §6` rule 1, §8 rule 1, ADR 0116 rule 19 amendment draft `docs/plan/0116-s36-amendment-draft.md`): iterations were counted across every `for ever` of the run and their end was a sticky host stop, so a `for ever` under a `secret` branch moved where a later `public` loop stopped and its output revealed the secret; each entry into a `for ever`, and the driver's loop, now counts its own iterations from 0 and stops at its own iteration `N + 1`, in both engines and in native code, without interrupting operations or reaching other loops (edition `0.4` only; no released version has the flag).
