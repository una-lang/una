- **Itaty speaks the client's owner formats and meets a registry's obligations** (#1144, ADR 0122 rules 36.4, 38 and 38.1, `spec/30 §14` rules 1 and 5, `§16`). The registry service reads every entry with the registry client's reader, so `ita-owner/1` with recovery cosigners and `ita-key/1` with every action (`add`, `revoke` with `since`, `cosigners`, `recover`, `cancel`, `overrule`, `dispute`) are the client's formats; an owner's state is the client's replay of its chain, run with each entry before it is logged, and an entry the replay refuses, cannot settle or leaves void is never logged, nor one that relies on a pending request before its deadline. Recoveries and disputes are logged only by the operator, after verifying the account (`POST /request`). Every checkpoint names its root metadata on one `ita-root` line; the log grows past a multiple of 64, a request's start, deadline or pre-deadline checkpoint only once it carries the witness threshold, and each request's deadline is the first checkpoint signed at or after its target, cosignatures that would move a checkpoint across a target refused. Witness rotation tooling lists revoked witnesses by key fingerprint, lists a compromised witness by default, never re-adds a revoked one, and refuses to raise the threshold while kept checkpoints fall short. Owners are notified of every key event, recovery and dispute through an append-only outbox the operator names, without which the service refuses to start. A round-trip test has the client verify an Itaty store with cosigners, a recovery, a dispute and a rotation.
