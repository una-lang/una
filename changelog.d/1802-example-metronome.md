- **`examples/metronome` and the driven programs guide** (#1802, `spec/26 §2`), edition 0.4 preview. The package names its module in a `drive` section of `ita.una`, with a tick every 500 milliseconds; `init`, `step` and `on_stop` count the beats of bars of four, and `una run . --stop-after-iterations 5` prints the same five beats and the stop in both engines. `docs/guide/driven-programs.md` explains the `drive` section, the three functions, stopping, testing and the mistakes the checker catches. The example and native differential tests now run a driven package with a stop after five steps.
