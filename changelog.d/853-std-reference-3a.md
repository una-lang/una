- **The standard library reference: the fixed-width integer methods** (#853). `docs/std/reference.json` now describes the conversions `to_i8` to `to_u64` and `to_int`, and the wrapping, saturating and bit operations and the shifts of the fixed-width integers, in the Numbers area, each with a checked example at the edges of the range. A conversion says from which types it widens and when it narrows with an `overflow` obligation. The shifts give their precondition, `0 <= by and by < w`, which the test ties to the rows of `spec/18 §5`.
