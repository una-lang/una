- **Started tasks that install handlers compare task by task in both engines** (#1542, `spec/27 §8` rule 4, `spec/16 §5`). Installations are numbered run-wide in the order they happen, so under the real clock two started tasks that both install handlers may interleave their numbers natively, where the interpreter gives each task its numbers in turn; the comparison of the two engines now sets aside one renumbering of the run's installations, a bijection that keeps each handler, and the spec says so. Under `--virtual-time` the whole traces stay equal. A new conformance case, `started_tasks_install_handlers`, and its native test lock both.
