- **`serve:` grants** (#1005, ADR 0123 step 3, `spec/31 §2`), edition 0.4 preview. A grant `serve:<scheme>://<address>:<port>`, with `;<item>` suffixes in `--grant` and item lines under a `serve` line in `ita.una`, names where a run may accept connections, with every limit of ADR 0123 rule 7 at its default unless the grant sets it: connections in all and per peer, handshakes, requests per connection, sizes of the request line, headers and body, byte budgets for bodies, replies and delivered requests, and the `idle`, `read`, `write` and `answer` times. Addresses are IP literals in canonical form, never names; overlapping grants are refused; `hosts` lists the names `Host` may carry, and without it only `localhost` and IP literals are accepted. Before `main`, the run raises its descriptor limit, opens the TLS certificate and key once each and checks the open descriptors (a regular file of at most 1 MiB; the key owned by the run's user or root, with no bit for others and no group write bit), and binds one listening socket per grant, with `IPV6_V6ONLY` and without `SO_REUSEPORT`. Errors are U7008 in `ita.una`, usage errors (exit code 2) for `--grant` and for the files and binding, and U7002 for a `serve:` grant in `una.toml`, in every edition. The TLS server configuration is pinned (TLS 1.3 only unless `tls12`, which requires the extended master secret) but not built, and the key is not yet checked against the certificate: both wait for the decision on the TLS provider (#1059). Nothing accepts on the listeners until the serve threads land.
