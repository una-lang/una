- **The renames of names that start with `_` stay linear in one large declaration** (#1635, ADR 0096 rule 12). The repairs of U0001 read each declaration once, into its words and their offsets, and compute each rename once per declaration and name, reused by every error of that name; the declaration of an error is found by a binary search over the declaration starts, read once per file, in these renames and in the exact rewrites of U0101. A function with `n` names `_x` took 0.24 s at 500 names and 15 s at 4,000; the pass is now O((n + e) log n + r) for `n` bytes, `e` errors and `r` bytes of repairs. The repairs themselves are unchanged.
