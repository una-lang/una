- **A step refused for the memory budget is no longer visible to the steps of sibling tasks** (#1943, `una-rt`). A step of a copy between tasks (ADR 0113 rule 4) added its bytes to the process-wide count of announced bytes before it was checked, and a refused step took them back only afterwards. In that interval the check of a step in a sibling task counted the refused bytes, so a step that fit the budget could be refused too, and the U6003 could be reported at the sibling instead of, or as well as, at the step that asked for too much. A step is now checked first and announced only when it fits, by a compare-exchange loop that is O(1) expected and lock-free, and the sum of the announced bytes can no longer wrap when several large steps are announced at once. No stop of a released program was observed.
