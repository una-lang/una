- **`fake_hash` is linear in its seed** (#1342, `spec/14 §4`, the complexity rule of the Proposed ADR for the standard library). `std.testing.fake_hash(seed)` no longer writes its seed in decimal: it hashes a fixed domain prefix and the seed's hash encoding of `spec/28 §1` rule 2 (sign and magnitude bytes, self-delimiting), in O(d) for a seed of d limbs. Equal seeds still give equal values and different seeds different values; the concrete digests change, but a `Hashed` value is observable only through `==`, so no output changes. A conformance case covers zero, signs, magnitudes and seeds past 64 bits, and a scaling test holds seeds of d and 10d limbs within 30 times.
