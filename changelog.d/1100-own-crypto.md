- **Own cryptography for TLS, slice 1** (#1100, ADR 0133, Proposed). A new crate, `una-crypto`, pure Rust with `unsafe` forbidden: SHA-256/384/512, HMAC, HKDF, ChaCha20-Poly1305 and X25519 in constant time, wiped on drop, checked against the RFC vectors, the vendored Wycheproof vectors (pinned by digest) and `ring` and `curve25519-dalek` as development references. `rustls` still uses `ring`; the provider switches in a later slice.
