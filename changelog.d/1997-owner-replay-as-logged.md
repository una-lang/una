- **The owner replay judges each entry as logged, and replays only key events** (#1997): each entry of an owner's chain is judged once against the chain as known when it was logged, and claims of compromise are settled in one pass from the last claim to the first; releases and yanks are judged as logged and never refuse a name, and a release or a yank its owner's keys as logged do not sign is skipped and recorded as evidence against the registry; contested chains count nothing; a claim signed by the key it names, or repeating a compromise already counting, is not logged; every owner keeps a threshold of at least two and at least one key more than its threshold, from 3 to 32 keys, after its registration and every key event, so a single stolen key signs nothing that counts (the floor counts keys, not keys that can still sign: an owner whose own claims leave a current key compromised, whose chain reaches the cap of claims, or with two keys stolen, registers under a new owner name with new keys); an owner chain holds at most 64 claims; owner chains that hold recoveries, disputes, co-signer changes, cancellations, overrulings or registrations with recovery cosigners are not replayed by this release and make the owner's names unavailable (U7013); the registry service reads owner entries with the client's parser and checks registrations, key events, releases and yanks with the client's rule (ADR 0122 Amendment 14, `spec/30` §14 to §16).
