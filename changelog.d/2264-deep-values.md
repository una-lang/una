- **No accepted program aborts with a stack overflow on a deep Core expression** (#2264, from the third review of #2261, lane S). The cap of 4,096 records, variants and lists that the module constants of one body may hold together (ADR 0126) now counts a whole test: its `given`, `do` and `expect` lines are one part, and elaboration shares each constant once in the Core expression of the `given` and `do` lines and once in each `expect` line. Three references to a constant of 4,095 records and lists in one `expect` line or one `given` line passed `una check` and then aborted `una test` with a stack overflow; they now run, and a test whose lines together name more is U2041. A declaration whose Core IR nests deeper than 131,072 levels (each statement, and each record, variant or list an expression builds, is one level) is now U0101 at check time (`spec/02 §9`), measured without recursion. Below that bound, every command runs: `una` runs each command, and the checker, on threads of 256 MiB of reserved stack instead of the 8 MiB main thread, and `una test` copies the program on the evaluator's stack. A written-out `[[0, 1], ...]` of 13,000 lists used to abort `una test`, and one of 32,000 lists also aborted `una core`, `una core --json` and `una build`; both now run.
