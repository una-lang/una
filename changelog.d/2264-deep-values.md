- **No accepted program aborts with a stack overflow on a deep Core expression** (#2264, from the third review of #2261, lane S). The cap of 4,096 records, variants and lists that the module constants of one body may hold together (ADR 0126) now counts a whole test: its `given`, `do` and `expect` lines are one part, and elaboration shares each constant once in the Core expression of the `given` and `do` lines and once in each `expect` line. Three references to a constant of 4,095 records and lists in one `expect` line or one `given` line passed `una check` and then aborted `una test` with a stack overflow; they now run, and a test whose lines together name more is U2041. A declaration whose Core IR nests deeper than 8,192 levels (a block of thousands of statements, a list literal of thousands of lists, as `[[0, 1], ...]` written out 13,000 times) is now U0101 at check time (`spec/02 §9`), where it used to abort `una test`, `una core`, `una report` or `una build`; the depth is measured without recursion. `una test` copies the program on the evaluator's stack.
