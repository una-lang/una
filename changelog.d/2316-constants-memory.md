- **A package of module constants that the caps reject no longer builds gigabytes first** (#2316, #2317, from the third review of #2278). The cap of 10,000,000 on what substituting module constants writes into a package now counts the decimal digits of an integer and of a decimal's coefficient, as it counts the characters of a text: a 4,300-digit integer named 100,000 times passed `una check` at 1.8 GB and is now U2041. A declaration that the cap rejects is left out of Core IR, as one past the depth bound is, and a list or record constant whose own substitution would pass the cap is U2041 at its value before it is built. The editor's hover of a constant is one text shared by its references, not a copy of the value at each one. Measured on a debug build, the review's probes that took 1.2 to 3.6 GB now take 25 to 200 MB. The caps of ADR 0126 rule 5 on one value are unchanged.
