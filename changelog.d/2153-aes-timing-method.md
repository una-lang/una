- **The cryptography's timing tests time the routine alone** (#2153): the AES-GCM seal test of `crates/una-crypto/tests/timing.rs` prepared its input inside the timed region, differently for its two classes, and the release gate `linux-arm64` failed on it (`aes-128-gcm seal t = -33.87`) with no dependence on the key or the plaintext in the implementation. That test, the two tag-rejection tests and the X25519 test now sample as the `ct::eq` test does: one state for both classes, written before the clock is read by the same work for both, and the first samples discarded. The claims and the limit are unchanged. No behavior of a program changes.
