- **The lexer does less work per word and per file** (#1894). A keyword is looked up by a `match` on the word instead of a scan of the table with a comparison per keyword; a lowercase word is read as a slice of the text instead of a copy; the names a file uses are collected only when a reserved word needs a rename; and the repairs of `_` names read the file's lines only when there is such an error. Tokens, errors and repairs are unchanged (tests pin the keyword table and that the standard modules take the path without the collection). `una check` of a small edition 0.2 package retires 2.0 million fewer instructions of 48.4 million (4.1%); `una check examples/hello` 1.1 million of 40.0 million (2.7%). The standard modules, lexed on every check, take most of it.
