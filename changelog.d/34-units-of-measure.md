- **Units of measure** (#34, ADR 0104, `spec/25`). In edition `0.3`, `measure` declares a unit (`pub measure Meter`, `measure Kilometer = 1000 * Meter`) and `of` attaches one to an integer type (`Int of Meter / Second`, `I64 of Millisecond`). A unit is part of the type: operands, arguments, results and comparisons agree on it (U2028), constants take the unit their context expects, `*` and `/` combine units, and a measured type satisfies `Eq` and `Ord` but not `Integer`. `measured()`, `magnitude()` and `convert()` are the only ways to change a unit, `convert()` only by an exact integer ratio that fits the magnitude type (U2030); an invalid unit declaration is U2029. Units are erased after the type check, so a program's output, its run time, its Core export and its verification queries are those of the same program without them. Editions `0.1` and `0.2` do not change.
