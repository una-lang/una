- **Strict framing for HTTP servers** (#1003, ADR 0123 step 1, `spec/31 §3` and §4). `una-http` gains `strict`, the server's own framing beside the client's reader, which does not change: CRLF line ends only; header lines with no folding, no whitespace before the colon, no control characters and no repair of a value that is not UTF-8; a `Connection` list of its own headers only; one `Content-Length` of 1 to 19 digits; `Transfer-Encoding: chunked` alone and never with `Content-Length`; chunk sizes of 1 to 16 hex digits with no extension; trailers within what the header section left of its limits; origin-form targets of RFC 3986 characters, decoded segment by segment with `.`, `..`, encoded `/`, `\` and control characters refused. No construct is added yet.
