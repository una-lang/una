- **Native `within` under `--virtual-time` is cut at the check point after a wait at a limit** (#1823, `spec/27 §5` rule 5, §8 rule 3). A wait for a refill at a `std.limit` limiter, or at the limits of an `Http` grant, moves the virtual clock, up to the covering deadline. The interpreter then cut `e` at its next check point, while an executable noticed the passed deadline only when `e` returned, so it still performed the effects in between and ran the tasks of a block after the wait. Every check point of an executable under `--virtual-time` now compares the clock with the earliest deadline that covers its task, own or inherited, so both engines give the same output and trace. Runs on the real clock are unchanged.
