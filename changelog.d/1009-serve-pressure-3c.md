- **Pressure and a program that holds most of its memory, alike in both engines** (#1009, ADR 0123 rule 13 and Conformance, `spec/31 §6` rule 6): under 64 paced connections that never read 1 MiB replies, 64 that never read 3 MiB replies and 64 that trickle 1 MiB bodies, a fresh request is answered and the connections furthest over their share are closed as replies are reserved; a program whose state takes more than 80% of `--max-memory` still delivers and answers; both give the same bytes, output and trace in the interpreter and in native code.
