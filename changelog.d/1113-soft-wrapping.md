- **Canonical soft wrapping in edition 0.4** (#1113, ADR 0125 and its amendment of 2026-10-25, `spec/15 §2`). In edition 0.4, `una fmt` and `una check` wrap a logical line wider than 100 Unicode scalar values after a comma between arguments or elements, at the outermost separator that fits, in time linear in the line's length. This is the canonical form of edition 0.4 only and is not backported: editions 0.1 to 0.3 keep their canonical form, so their code gets no new U7001, and their obligation ids, goals and hypotheses are unchanged. In edition 0.4, obligation goals and hypotheses and `una contract` show source text without the wrapped layout, so wrapping a line changes no obligation id.
