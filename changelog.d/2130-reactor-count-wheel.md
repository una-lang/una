- **The timing wheel counts the steps of `arm` and `cancel`, and the reactor's scaling test counts an insert into a freed slot** (#2130, `crates/una-http/src/wheel.rs`, `crates/una-rt/src/reactor.rs`; follow-ups of #2124). Tests only: outside the unit tests the wheel's slab and free list are `Vec` itself, through a type alias, as the reactor's table already was. In the unit tests of `una-http` each access to the wheel's slab and free list counts one step, and a new test holds `arm` to 7 steps (5 into a freed entry) and `cancel` to 7 (1 for a timer that is gone) at 1,000 to 100,000 timers that share one slot, with the total exactly those constants times the timers; a scan added to either fails it on any machine, where before only a wall-clock bound of the reactor's test saw one. The reactor's test registers a second time after its removes, with the free list at its longest, and holds that `insert` to 4 steps. A test of each type names every field and its type, so a collection added to the reactor's table or to the wheel does not compile until it is listed, and the documentation of both states that a collection that grows must be counted. The reactor's test no longer says that the wheel is counted there: it is counted in its own crate, by the ordinary tests, and not under Miri or AddressSanitizer. `scripts/sanitize.sh` now says that the timing of the reactor's test does not run under either.
