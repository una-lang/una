- **A program that handles stops runs under a soft limit of open files at or below 512** (#1757). The wake pipe of the stop handlers moved to descriptor 512 or above, which `fcntl` refuses under a lower limit, so every such run, under launchd's default of 256 for one, ended with the internal error U8001; the pipe now moves as high as the limit allows, and a limit below 16 is a usage error, in both engines.
