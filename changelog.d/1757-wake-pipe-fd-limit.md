- **A program that handles stops runs under a soft limit of open files below 514** (#1757). The wake pipe of the stop handlers moved to descriptor 512 or above, which `fcntl` refuses at or above the limit, so every such run under a limit of 513 or less, launchd's default of 256 among them, ended with the internal error U8001; the pipe now moves as high as the limit allows, never onto a standard stream, and a limit below 16 or too few free descriptors is a usage error, in both engines.
