- **Profiles and the declared memory budget** (#31, ADR 0109 rules 1, 4, 6 and 7). A package declares its profile, `app`, the default, or `systems`, and its memory budget, from 1 to 4096 MiB: `[package] profile` and `[run] max_memory` in `una.toml` of edition `0.3`, the head line `profile` and the `run` line `max_memory` in `ita.una` of edition `0.4`, where `profile systems` is now accepted. A `systems` package with an entry declares its budget (U7002, U7008). The declared budget is the budget of `una run`, `una test` and the executable `una build` makes; `--max-memory` may lower it, and a value above it is a usage error (exit code 2). The build report and the Core export name the profile, and U2039 and U3032 are registered for the `simd required` clause of ADR 0120. Editions `0.1` and `0.2` are unchanged.
