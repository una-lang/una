- **Verifying a registry's documents** (#893, ADR 0122 rules 35 to 39, `spec/30 §11`). The primitives of the registry client: RFC 9162 inclusion and consistency proofs, with a test on logs of up to 2^30 leaves that the work grows by one hash per doubling; strict Ed25519 (canonical `S`, keys and `R` neither non-canonical nor of small order, the cofactorless equation); C2SP signed notes, checkpoints and cosignatures with canonical base64; and witness time as order statistics over the witness threshold. Ed25519 is `ed25519-dalek`'s strict verification, pure Rust with no C, behind this module's own checks of canonical encodings and small-order points. No command fetches yet.
