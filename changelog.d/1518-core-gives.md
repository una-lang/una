- **`una core` exports operation labels, and the formal re-check no longer accepts a labeled operation** (#1518, ADR 0094 rule 8, `spec/13 §8`). A `uses` entry of `una.core/1` holds `gives`, after `ops`, for each operation its binding labels, declared or inferred, left out when none is labeled, so documents of packages without labels are unchanged. The re-check reports a declaration whose `uses` label an operation, a handler's operation among them, and what reaches it, as not re-checked, "F1 restriction (a labeled operation, ADR 0094 rule 8)", until F1 models operation labels (#563): before, the export wrote no label and such a declaration could be reported accepted under H1 to H3, without the premise H5 it rests on.
