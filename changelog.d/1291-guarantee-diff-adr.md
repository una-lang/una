- **Guarantee diff and specification mutation, proposed** (#1291, #1293, ADR 0128 and ADR 0129 Proposed): `una diff` compares a package with a baseline snapshot and lists what changed in what a reader can trust (contracts by implication where the solver decides, otherwise `review_required` or `inconclusive`; authority, `trusted`, `checked`, declassifications, obligations, the solver pin, requirements), with approvals bound to one change in `guarantees.toml` and a strict `--gate`. Specification mutation testing reports the test mutation score and the verifier rejection rate apart, with their denominators, measures specification sensitivity, and adds solver-decided trivial-contract checks. Nothing is implemented.
