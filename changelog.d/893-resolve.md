- **Minimal version selection** (#893, ADR 0122 rules 20 to 24, `spec/30 §10`). The resolver of edition `0.4`: for each identity, the highest of the minimum versions the reached needs ask for, never the newest; raises past yanked, advised and avoided releases that wait the 72-hour cooldown, which only a threshold-signed advisory lifts for its fix; every bound of rule 23; and a scaling test on random graphs of up to 100,000 releases. It runs in memory; no command reads a registry yet.
