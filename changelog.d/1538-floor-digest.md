- **Security: the root-version floor binds the digest of its root metadata** (#1538, ADR 0122 amendment of 2026-11-09, L3). Itaoca stores the root-version floor of a registry as `<n> sha256:<hex>`, the version and the digest of that root metadata, still replaced only by a higher version. Every offline load and `una ita unbundle` refuse (U7010) a provenance or bundle whose root chain holds version `n` with another digest, a fork of the root chain, and the registry client refuses a walked chain the same way. A floor of the earlier form, the version alone, is still read and compared by version only; a floor file of neither form cannot be read and makes an offline load unavailable (U7013). Storing the floor stays a note on failure. Cost: O(R) more per load for R root versions.
