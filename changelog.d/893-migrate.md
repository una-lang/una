- **`una migrate`** (#893, ADR 0122 rules 63 and 64, `spec/30 §8`). It moves a package from `una.toml` and `verification.lock`, of any edition from `0.1`, to `ita.una` and `ita.lock` of edition `0.4`: names with `-` become `_` (`--name` for others), every table becomes its section, grants become their lines, and comments are carried above the line they precede or listed. It then prints the diagnostics the change of edition causes, applying none; `--dry-run` writes nothing and `--json` prints `una.migrate/1`. `una.toml` naming edition `0.4` is now U7002, with `una migrate` as its repair.
