- **`Complex[F64]` and `Complex[F32]` in edition 0.4** (#986, ADR 0120 rules 22 to 25 and V3, `spec/29 §19`). Two built-in types with the fields `re` and `im`, built by `complex(re: x, im: y)`, whose two arguments are both `F64` or both `F32` (U2001 otherwise); `Complex` of any other type is U2002. `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, unary `-`, `conj()`, `scale(by:)` and `norm_sqr()` are defined by the operations of the components, in a fixed order and with no contraction, so both engines compute the same bits; `/` is Smith's algorithm, with its documented loss when the ratio of the divisor's components underflows. `==` compares both components by identity. A complex and a real operand are U2001, with repairs to `scale(by:)` and to `complex(re: x, im: 0.0)`; `<` and `mod` are U2001. `to_text`, interpolation and the Una form write `complex(re: 1.0, im: -2.0)`. In contracts, `re`, `im`, `complex(re:, im:)` and `==` are in the refinement language, encoded as an uninterpreted sort with its two field functions; the arithmetic and the methods are U2014. A complex value carries the labels of its components. A package's own `complex` keeps its meaning.
