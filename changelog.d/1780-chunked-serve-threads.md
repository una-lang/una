- **Chunked request bodies on many serve threads** (#1780, ADR 0123 rules 12 and 13). A chunked body no longer allocates once a chunk: its size lines share one buffer, so it allocates only as its buffers grow, O(log b) times for b bytes, and serve threads no longer contend for the process's counting allocator on bodies of small chunks (1-byte chunks, 8 connections: 8 serve threads took 233 ms against 118 ms on 1 before, and now about 42 ms against 82 ms). A connection that used up its steps of one pump while bytes it had read were not fed yet is now pumped again on its thread's next pass; before, it waited for the socket to signal again, which a peer whose request was complete never made happen, and was answered `408` when its `read` timer ran out.
