- **spec/30 states the root-version floor with its digest** (#1538, ADR 0122 amendment of 2026-11-09, L3; `spec/30 §9` rule 7, §14 Floors, §15). The specification now gives the floor file's format, `<n> sha256:<hex>` and exactly one line feed, the rule that only a higher version replaces it, the earlier form `<n>` compared by version only, a file in neither form as a floor that cannot be read (U7013 offline), and the refusal (U7010) of a root chain that holds the floor's version with another digest, a fork, by the offline load, `una ita unbundle` and the registry client. Documentation only; the behavior shipped in slice 4.
