- **`for ever within` and the tick of a driven step take the label of the work beside them** (#1087, ADR 0116 amendment of 2026-10-25, C, slice 5 of the precise `τ_conc`, `spec/26 §2` rule 5 and §7 rule 5, `spec/27 §11`). A `for ever within` that a part of a task construct can reach now takes `κ` of the constructs around it, instead of `⊤`, and the tick's `input` needs `conc(step)`, the labels of the work beside every call of `step`: a step called beside tasks on `public` data needs no label on its tick.
