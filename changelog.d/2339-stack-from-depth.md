- **Unoptimized builds no longer overflow their stack below the Core depth bound** (#2339). Every thread of `una` that walks Core IR by recursion (the command's, the checker's and the verifier's, the copy of the program for `una test`, and the tier 1 check of `una lsp`) now has a stack sized from the bound: 131,072 levels at 8 KiB each, plus 64 MiB, 1,088 MiB reserved and not committed. 8 KiB is twice the most any pass took per level in an unoptimized build (opt-level 0, the build of the release gates), which is `una core`'s printer at about 4 KiB; x86-64 frames are within 5% of aarch64 frames. At opt-level 0, a body of 126,000 levels aborted `una test` and `una core` on aarch64 with the 256 MiB stacks of #2264, as on x86-64 Linux; both now pass.
