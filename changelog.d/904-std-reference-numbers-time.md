- **The standard library reference describes the floating-point functions of edition `0.3`, their JSON conversions and the `std.time` constants** (#904, part 4 of #853, first slice). `docs/std/reference.json` gains `nan`, `infinity`, `f64_from_bits`, `parse_f64`, `as_f64`, `json_number`, and `std.time` `millisecond`, `second` and `minute`, each with an example. An example of an item that edition `0.2` lacks is now a package of the item's first edition: a `una.toml` of edition `0.3`, or from edition `0.4` an `ita.una` with its `ita.lock`, which the test of the reference checks as it checks the manifest of edition `0.2`.
