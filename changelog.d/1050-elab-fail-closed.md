- **Security: a declaration that does not elaborate to Core IR rejects the package (U8001)** (#1050). A declaration whose elaboration to Core IR stopped without a diagnostic, such as when a resolution the records do not hold is looked up, was left out of Core IR with none of its obligations, so its `requires`, `ensures`, refinements and arithmetic checks were never verified, yet the package was accepted. Elaboration now ends with a cross-check: every type, function, predicate, policy, capability, handler and example of the package that type-checked must be in Core IR, and every contract of the standard library, host capability and prelude kind that Core IR carries must have elaborated; one that is missing is the internal error U8001 and the package is rejected, unless another error already rejects it. A refinement clause that does not elaborate now leaves its declaration out too, so the cross-check finds it, instead of standing in Core IR as a placeholder `false`. No declaration of the prelude, the standard modules or the conformance corpus is dropped today.
