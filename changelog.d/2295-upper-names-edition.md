- **A dependency of edition `0.4` no longer sees the upper-case prelude names of edition `0.5` in the type check** (#2295, ADR 0122 rule 49; a change of the edition `0.5` preview, for the heading "Edition `0.5` preview: changes that may need edits" of ADR 0121 rule 6; kind: U2001, U2003, U2029). Under a root of edition `0.5`, the type checker of a dependency of edition `0.4` still read `F32`, `Finite64`, `Finite32`, `Complex`, `Rounding` and the variants of `Rounding` as the built-ins of edition `0.5` after the name resolution had refused them with U1001, so the dependency got diagnostics it does not get alone: an extra U2001 at `kind Positive = F32 where it > 0` and at a `Finite64` returned as a `Finite32`, an extra U2003 at `Complex`, and the U2029 of `Int of Complex` said "`Complex` is not a unit" instead of "unknown unit `Complex`". Now the type checker hides those names from a module of an edition before `0.5`, as the name resolution does, so a dependency gives exactly what it gives alone. `Array` and `Decimal` stay visible there: in edition `0.4` they are variants of `Json`. Every such program was refused before and is refused now. The cost is one edition comparison per upper-case name that falls back to the prelude, O(1).
