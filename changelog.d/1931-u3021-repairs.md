- **U3021 names the caller's binding and the handler that U3020 offers** (#1931, ADR 0093 rule 6, `spec/13 §3`). The narrowed grant of U3021's repair names the caller's binding, as U3020's does, instead of a placeholder `@y`; when several bindings qualify it lists them. When the callee declares an operation the ceiling withholds, the repair says that no grant of a binding keeps both and names a handler whose own `uses` lie within the ceiling, the repair U3020 gives for the same requirement, before leaving the choice to the consumer. The codes and the messages do not change.
