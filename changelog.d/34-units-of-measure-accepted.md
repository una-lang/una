- **Accepted, not yet implemented: units of measure** (#34, ADR 0104, accepted on 2026-10-14). In edition `0.3`, `measure` will declare a unit (`pub measure Meter`, `measure Kilometer = 1000 * Meter`) and `of` will attach one to an integer type (`Int of Meter / Second`, `I64 of Millisecond`). A unit will be part of the type: operands, arguments and results will agree on it (U2028), and `measured()`, `magnitude()` and `convert()` will be the only ways to change it, `convert()` only by an exact integer ratio. Units will be erased after the type check, so a program's output, its run time and its verification queries will be those of the same program without them. Editions `0.1` and `0.2` will not change.
