- **Release archives without `lib/`** (#1207, ADR 0059's amendment): the archive holds `una`, `LICENSE`, `README.md` and the guides; `scripts/check-release.sh` expects exactly those, refuses a `lib/` entry, scans the embedded runtime once its digest is the packed `una`'s, and builds `examples/hello` with `UNA_RT_LIB` and runtime files beside `una` that must change nothing; `install.sh` installs no `lib/` from such an archive, still installs the `lib/libuna_rt.a` of an earlier release, keeps an earlier installation's runtime in its record for uninstall, and refuses an archive with any other entry.
