- **The language server bounds the frames it reads** (#1476, security, ADR 0063 rule 2): `una lsp` read a header line with no bound, accepted any number of header lines and reserved a body of any `Content-Length` before reading it, so a peer on its input could make it hold memory without limit. A header line now holds at most 4 KiB, a frame at most 16 header lines and a body at most 64 MiB; input past a bound is refused before anything of its size is buffered or reserved, and the session ends as at the end of the input. Real editors are not affected: their headers are under 100 bytes and their documents far below the body limit.
