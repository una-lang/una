- **Big integer follow-ups** (#1368, after #1093). Products whose shorter operand has more than 9/16 of the longer one's limbs take Toom-3 from 256 limbs, where Karatsuba was up to 15% slower than the num-bigint baseline; `Int` and `BigUint` operands of at most two limbs divide in 128-bit arithmetic (a 2-limb by 1-limb division was 1.45x the baseline, now 0.35x to 0.77x); reading decimal text keeps Horner's rule up to 14,592 digits; writing digits divides by radix powers without their zero low limbs, 7% to 11% faster on random values from 128 limbs. Results are unchanged bit for bit. `docs/plan/1093-bigint-design.md` now states the measured ratios, including the cases still slower than num-bigint (powers of ten written as text, 1.05x to 2.50x). An allocation of an impossible size reports the layout that was asked for. ADR 0004, 0055 and 0120's sections of #1093 are headed as amendments.
