- **An obligation's goal over decimals shows decimal values** (#1041, `spec/29 §16` rule 11): a goal, its message and its repairs show the clause as written, `result >= 0.00` for `it >= 0.00` rather than the coefficient `result >= 0`, with no aligned coefficient and with `coefficient()` named, as a counterexample already shows values; a clause with a comment, or with a number argument before `.coefficient()`, still shows coefficients (`spec/29 §16` rule 14). The query is unchanged; the ids of the obligations whose goal holds a decimal literal or `coefficient()` change with their goal text.
