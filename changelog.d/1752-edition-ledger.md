- **The edition ledger** (#1752, part 1, ADR 0121 rule 9, as its amendment of 2026-11-06 extends it to every preview edition): the compiler holds one table, beside `Edition` in `una-project`, with an entry for each construct of editions `0.4` (ADRs 0116, 0117, 0122 and 0123) and `0.5` (ADRs 0119 and 0120): its record, its reserved and contextual keywords, the prelude names, built-in methods, standard modules and variants it adds, its project-file sections and codes, its clashes with the edition before by ledger id, its trust, its unsettled points and the release that landed it. The guide's page on editions lists the constructs of the preview editions, and a test checks that list, and the words the lexer reserves for edition `0.4`, against the ledger. The ledger is a static table: an entry is found by its construct in O(1). The report's `preview_constructs`, which reads it, follows in part 2. Behavior is unchanged.
