- **Per-name and per-zone bounds on the `Http` lookup and connect pools** (#1265, `spec/24 §4` rule 2, ADR 0117 slice S7 review R7-10 and R7-13). One lookup per name runs at a time, names compared without case, and every other request of the name joins it, each until its own deadline, so a name whose lookups stall holds one thread of the lookup pool instead of all of them. Each pool admits at most a quarter of its threads, and at least 1, to one zone (a name's last two labels, an IPv4 `/24`, an IPv6 `/48`); a step of a zone at its share waits in its zone's own line and holds no other zone behind it. Steps whose requests gave up are pruned once a line doubles, so a pool holds O(waiting requests + threads) entries however long its steps hang, at an amortized O(1) per step. A lookup that panics fails with `Unreachable` and keeps its pool thread.
