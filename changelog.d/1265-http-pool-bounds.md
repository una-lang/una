- **Per-name and per-zone bounds on the `Http` lookup and connect pools** (#1265, `spec/24 §4` rule 2, ADR 0117 slice S7 review R7-10 and R7-13). Requests of one name that wait at the same time share one lookup, names compared without case, each until its own deadline; a lookup whose requests all gave up takes no new request, so a later request starts its own lookup and residue never answers it. Each pool admits at most a quarter of its threads, and at least 1, to one zone (a name's last two labels, an IPv4 `/24` with IPv4-mapped IPv6 addresses included, another IPv6 `/48`), residue included; a step of a zone at its share waits in its zone's own line and holds no other zone behind it, and zones are refilled first come, first served. So a stalled name or zone holds at most a quarter of a pool instead of all of it. Steps whose requests gave up are pruned once a line doubles, so each line holds at most twice what it kept at its last prune, or 32: O(peak waiting requests + threads), at an amortized O(1) per step. A step that panics fails its request at once with `Unreachable` and keeps its pool thread. Open channel, stated in `spec/24 §4` and `spec/26 §7` rule 5: a lookup or connection left running by a deadline's cut keeps its zone's place, so at a zone share of 1 it can delay a later request of its zone past that request's deadline, and `L_res` does not yet count it.
