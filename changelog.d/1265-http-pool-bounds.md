- **One lookup per name on the `Http` lookup pool, and bounded pool lines** (#1265, `spec/24 §4` rule 2, ADR 0117 slice S7 review R7-10): requests of one name that wait at the same time share one lookup thread, names compared without case, each until its own deadline, and a lookup whose requests all gave up never answers a later request; lines are pruned of given-up steps once they double, so a pool holds O(peak waiting requests + threads) steps and names at an amortized O(1) per step; a step that panics fails its request at once with `Unreachable`; the per-zone bound is left to #1717.
