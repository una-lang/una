- **The formal model covers ADR 0116** (#946, `spec/26 §9`). F1 gains Core's exits (`stop` and `skip`), `for until` and `for ever` with `on stop`, and the host's stop schedule. T1 and T6 cover them, T6 for every fixed stop schedule; new theorems prove that a decreasing measure ends a `for until` and that a host stop ends a `for ever` at its stop point; erasure, host independence, authority confinement and trace safety extend to them. The re-check of `una.core/1` reads and checks the new forms: a `stop` or `skip` above the public program counter, and a `for until` condition that may fail, are reported as not re-checked, never accepted, and a `for ever within` is not re-checked, since deadlines are timing. The host-driven step is modeled under a fixed schedule as a loop over the ticks it delivers. `formal/Theorems.lean` states the new theorems, with no `sorry` and no axiom beyond Lean's own.
