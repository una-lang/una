- **Positive controls for the serve threads' timing channels** (#1660, ADR 0123 S4 design §11.5). The release timing oracles `serve_handoff_residue` and `serve_delivery_history` show on the machine that pending reply bytes and the time between deliveries under a rate change whether a later `next` under a deadline receives its request, the channels that `L_srv` labels; their `secret` versions are conformance cases refused with U4003.
