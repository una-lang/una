- **Native `next` waits on the reactor, not on a helper of the I/O pool** (#1723, `spec/27 §2` rule 3, `spec/31 §1`). A task of native code waiting in `next` held a helper of the `--io-threads` pool for its whole wait, which `Http`'s lookups and connections need too: with more waiters than helpers, or with every helper taken by `Http` calls to a slow backend, requests got `503` or delivery stopped for every request until a connect timed out. The wait now parks the task on the reactor with its wake left with the serve engine, which wakes one waiter per request and every waiter at a stop; an idle waiter holds no thread and is woken by no timer, only by a request, a stop, its deadline, its cancellation or an abort of the run; such a wait arms no timer on the reactor, and the macOS poller cuts a timeout to the `INT32_MAX` seconds `kevent` takes, so a far timer never fails its waits.
