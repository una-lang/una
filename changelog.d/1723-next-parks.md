- **Native `next` waits on the reactor, not on a helper of the I/O pool** (#1723, `spec/27 §2` rule 3, `spec/31 §1`). A task of native code waiting in `next` held a helper of the `--io-threads` pool for its whole wait, which `Http`'s lookups and connections need too: with more waiters than helpers, or with every helper taken by `Http` calls to a slow backend, requests got `503` or delivery stopped for every request until a connect timed out. The wait now parks the task on the reactor with its wake left with the serve engine, which keeps one wake per request, so waiting tasks hold no thread and both engines answer the same.
