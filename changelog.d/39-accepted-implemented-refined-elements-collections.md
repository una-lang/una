- **Accepted, not yet implemented: refined elements of collections in edition `0.3`** (#39, ADR 0103, accepted on 2026-10-14). `List[Pos]`, `Option[Pos]`, `Map[Text, Pos]` and other refined type arguments, U2002 today by ADR 0040, will be well-formed in edition `0.3`. Every flow of a value into a refined position will be a `refinement` obligation: each element of a list literal, the arguments of `append`, `set`, `or_else` and a map's `with` and `set`, the results of `map`'s and `fold`'s functions, and a whole collection, compared by variance over a fresh binder. Every element read through `first`, `at`, a pattern, a loop variable, a lambda's parameter or a map's `get` will carry the refinement as a fact. Every query stays quantifier-free. Editions `0.1` and `0.2` do not change. Edition `0.3` will accept programs that edition `0.2` rejects, under an amendment of the edition's preview rule that the record makes. Nothing is implemented yet.
