- **Real-clock races are tested for being runs the specification allows** (#1516, `crates/una-cli/tests/native.rs`, `docs/compiler/testing.md`). On the real clock a native `first of` runs its branches concurrently, so a losing branch may print before the winner returns and may or may not reach its `cancelled` event, where the interpreter runs the branches in index order and never starts the loser: both are outcomes `spec/27 §6` rules 3, 4 and 6, `spec/19 §6` rule 4 and ADR 0117 rule 12 allow, and under `--virtual-time` the engines agree exactly, as before. No engine changes. New tests run five programs of races (a loser about to print, a loser that has finished, three branches, a loser that computes, nested races) fifty times each natively, with one worker and with several, and once in the interpreter, and hold every run to the rules no schedule changes: the winner's events first, the other branches' in index order, at most one `cancelled` event as a loser's last, the output's lines those of the events, and `seq` with no gap.
