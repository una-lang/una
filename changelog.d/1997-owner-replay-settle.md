- **Owner replay: settle claims that void their own signers** (#1997). The replay of an owner's chain drops a claim of compromise that voids its own signers before it assumes the claims together, settles the claims in log order so that a dropped claim never comes back, orders the drops of each pass, and counts a mutual signer for the own-word floor; `una ita publish` and `yank` also sign with the owner's former keys that `.una/keys` holds (ADR 0122 Amendment 13; `spec/30 §14` rule 5). The exhaustive model reports every failure it finds, checks depth 4 with a wider alphabet, and an ignored test runs random walks to depth 8.
