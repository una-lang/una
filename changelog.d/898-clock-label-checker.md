- **`Clock` readings carry their timing label** (#898, ADR 0116 Proposed amendment of 2026-11-09, `spec/14 §3`), edition 0.4 preview, as a reversible default. A reading of `Clock.now` is labeled with `τ⁰` of every ancestor of its site, joined with `τ_conc`, `L_res` and, in a driven package, the bodies of `init`, `step` and `on_stop`. Printing the elapsed time of work on `internal` or `secret` data is now U4003; a run on `public` data is unchanged, and an `internal` reading may be written with `Files.write`. The trace redacts a reading above `public` in both engines, and `una report` lists it under `information.timing` as `Clock.now`. Editions 0.1 to 0.3 do not change.
